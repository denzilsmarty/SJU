# Three-Phase Implementation Roadmap

This article describes the three-phase roadmap for implementing the Ignatian Pedagogical Paradigm (IPP) at **Saint Joseph's University (SJU)**, as outlined in the draft source-of-truth document *Forming the Future* (Version 0.1, 23 August 2026). The roadmap is paired with a set of governance guardrails that apply across all phases.[^chunk:514c92fa-878f-5bb4-b7dd-a668e30ffd19]

---

## Overview

The roadmap divides IPP implementation into three sequential phases: (1) establishing shared meaning, (2) testing in authentic settings, and (3) integrating and sustaining the framework. Each phase has a distinct focus, a defined set of activities, and implied leadership responsibilities. No explicit calendar durations are assigned to any phase in the source document; the roadmap is framed as a structure **for discussion** rather than as a promulgated policy.[^chunk:514c92fa-878f-5bb4-b7dd-a668e30ffd19]

---

## Phase 1: Establish Shared Meaning

### What happens

Phase 1 is foundational. Its purpose is to build a common institutional vocabulary and resolve questions of authority before any large-scale pedagogical change is attempted. The specific activities listed are:[^chunk:514c92fa-878f-5bb4-b7dd-a668e30ffd19]

- **Designate a cross-functional steward and editorial group** to own and maintain the source-of-truth document.
- **Review the draft** with faculty governance, the Mission office, academic leadership, student-facing divisions, assessment, accessibility staff, and students.
- **Resolve terminology and approve an authority policy** for sources (the document provides a four-tier source hierarchy to guide this work).
- **Produce a concise public primer** derived from the full guide, for broader campus use.
- **Identify where existing SJU practice already embodies the paradigm**, so the institution builds on what is already working.

### Who leads

The primary leadership structure created in Phase 1 is the **cross-functional steward** and an **editorial group**. Faculty governance is explicitly listed as a key consultative body in the review process.[^chunk:514c92fa-878f-5bb4-b7dd-a668e30ffd19] The document does not name a specific individual or office as steward; the owner/steward field in the document control section is listed as "To be designated."[^chunk:514c92fa-878f-5bb4-b7dd-a668e30ffd19]

### Duration

No specific timeframe is given for Phase 1 in the source document.[^chunk:514c92fa-878f-5bb4-b7dd-a668e30ffd19]

## Phase 2: Test in Authentic Settings

### What happens

Phase 2 moves from conceptual alignment to grounded experimentation. Its activities are:[^chunk:514c92fa-878f-5bb4-b7dd-a668e30ffd19]

- **Support voluntary pilots** across different schools, modalities, and co-curricular units.
- **Fund course and process redesign**, not merely workshops — emphasizing sustained investment over one-time training events.
- **Gather baseline and implementation evidence** to build an empirical record of how the IPP operates in SJU's specific context.
- **Include community partners and students** in appropriate evaluation activities.
- **Document tensions, adaptations, and counterexamples** so that learning from difficulty is preserved alongside success stories.

### What 'voluntary pilots' means and who can participate

The phrase "voluntary pilots" signals that participation in Phase 2 experimentation is **not compulsory**. This language is consistent with the document's broader commitment to invitational, developmental implementation and its explicit warning that "faculty formation should not become a loyalty test."[^chunk:514c92fa-878f-5bb4-b7dd-a668e30ffd19] The pilots are designed to span **different schools, modalities, and co-curricular units**, meaning that potential participants include faculty across academic disciplines, staff responsible for advising, campus ministry, residence life, athletics, career services, and community engagement offices — in both on-campus and online or hybrid formats.[^chunk:514c92fa-878f-5bb4-b7dd-a668e30ffd19] The emphasis on funding "course and process redesign" rather than workshops suggests that participants are expected to invest real design work, not simply attend an information session.

### Who leads

The document does not name a specific Phase 2 leader. By extension from Phase 1, the cross-functional steward and editorial group would coordinate this phase, with faculty governance remaining a key consultative partner.[^chunk:514c92fa-878f-5bb4-b7dd-a668e30ffd19]

### Duration

No specific timeframe is given for Phase 2 in the source document.[^chunk:514c92fa-878f-5bb4-b7dd-a668e30ffd19]

## Phase 3: Integrate and Sustain

### What happens

Phase 3 embeds the IPP into ongoing institutional structures without imposing a single method. Its activities are:[^chunk:514c92fa-878f-5bb4-b7dd-a668e30ffd19]

- **Incorporate the framework** into faculty and staff formation, program review, and selected design processes.
- **Align recognition, workload, and resources** with the educational work the IPP requires — addressing the structural conditions that determine whether change is sustainable.
- **Create exemplars** from SJU's own disciplines and units, building a locally grounded body of practice.
- **Connect IPP evidence to existing assessment and accreditation processes**, integrating IPP-generated data into the accountability structures SJU already operates within.
- **Review the source-of-truth document on a published cycle**, maintaining the document's integrity and currency over time.

### How Phase 3 connects IPP evidence to accreditation

The roadmap explicitly names connecting IPP evidence to "existing assessment and accreditation processes" as a Phase 3 activity.[^chunk:514c92fa-878f-5bb4-b7dd-a668e30ffd19] The logic is that evidence gathered during Phase 2 pilots — student work, course and program assessment, belonging and access data, qualitative accounts, partner feedback, faculty learning, and alumni outcomes — becomes the evidentiary base that can be channeled into program review and accreditation reporting.[^chunk:a6c62be7-4932-5dfc-b4ea-52911d9de940] This approach avoids creating a parallel, standalone IPP reporting system; instead, IPP evidence is woven into assessments SJU is already required to conduct. The document emphasizes using "calibrated claims" so that only evidence-supported assertions about IPP effects enter these processes.[^chunk:a6c62be7-4932-5dfc-b4ea-52911d9de940]

### Who leads

Phase 3 activities are distributed across the institution: the cross-functional steward maintains the document; program review processes involve academic units and accreditation offices; formation activities involve faculty and staff development units; and resource alignment involves academic leadership and administration.[^chunk:514c92fa-878f-5bb4-b7dd-a668e30ffd19]

### Duration

No specific timeframe is given for Phase 3. The document control section recommends an **annual review cycle during development** and **at least every three years after adoption** for the source-of-truth document itself, suggesting Phase 3 is understood as an ongoing, open-ended condition rather than a bounded project.[^chunk:514c92fa-878f-5bb4-b7dd-a668e30ffd19]

## Governance Guardrails (All Phases)

The roadmap is accompanied by guardrails that apply throughout implementation:[^chunk:514c92fa-878f-5bb4-b7dd-a668e30ffd19]

- SJU **should not certify individuals as "Ignatian"** based on a short course.
- SJU **should not require performative mission language** in every syllabus.
- SJU **should not use the IPP rubric for personnel judgment** without validated purpose, due process, and contextual interpretation.
- Implementation should be **invitational, developmental, and evidence-generating** while institutional expectations remain clear where mission responsibilities are formally assigned.

These guardrails reflect the document's broader insistence that the paradigm must be enacted through genuine practice, not compliance performance.[^chunk:9a34ed4d-6e02-5417-886f-cc974e519300]
