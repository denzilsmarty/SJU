## Overview

The Initiative for Human Formation in an Age of AI is a University-wide, multi-year commitment announced by Saint Joseph's University to prepare students, faculty, and staff for a future shaped by artificial intelligence.[^chunk:81794096-93b3-5e4c-81e8-eede3fdc05fc] The initiative was launched in response to the rapid transformation that AI is bringing to education, work, and professional practice.[^chunk:1db83593-eb35-5650-ad0d-fdf970607ec3]

President Cheryl A. McConnell, PhD, emphasized that this is not a marginal technology initiative but rather a fundamental commitment grounded in the University's mission: "Artificial intelligence is changing how students learn, how faculty teach, how employees work and the professions graduates will enter. Saint Joseph's is responding by strengthening what matters most in today's environment: human judgment and the formation of the whole person along with rigorous academic preparation for the workplace of tomorrow."[^chunk:81794096-93b3-5e4c-81e8-eede3fdc05fc]

## Why the Initiative Was Launched

Artificial intelligence is reshaping the workplace faster than any previous technology, changing entry-level jobs, shifting employer expectations, and transforming which skills are valued.[^chunk:1db83593-eb35-5650-ad0d-fdf970607ec3] The initiative was developed following several months of consultation with the Board of Trustees, alumni, faculty, staff, students, and regional governmental and business leaders, and draws on Pope Leo XIV's encyclical *Magnifica Humanitas: "On Safeguarding the Human Person in the Time of Artificial Intelligence."*[^chunk:81794096-93b3-5e4c-81e8-eede3fdc05fc]

## Three Interconnected Focus Areas

The Initiative takes shape across three interconnected areas:[^chunk:81794096-93b3-5e4c-81e8-eede3fdc05fc]

1. **Faculty Preparation**: Preparing faculty to teach and innovate in an AI-shaped environment
2. **Student Experience**: Deepening the student experience around core human capacities such as emotional intelligence, ethical reasoning, critical and creative thinking, and problem identification and framing
3. **Institutional Capabilities**: Building the institutional capabilities that allow faculty and staff to use AI thoughtfully, responsibly, and effectively

## Key Programs and Resources

### AI Innovation Studio

The Initiative includes an AI Innovation Studio: a new collaborative space where faculty, staff, students, and community partners come together to explore how AI and human ingenuity can address complex, real-world challenges.[^chunk:81794096-93b3-5e4c-81e8-eede3fdc05fc] The studio fosters interdisciplinary and community collaboration, experimentation, and the development of practical solutions that put human needs and values at the center of the work.[^chunk:1db83593-eb35-5650-ad0d-fdf970607ec3]

### Horizon Series

Saint Joseph's hosts an ongoing Horizon Series, bringing together industry leaders and experts for discipline-clustered conversations about how AI is reshaping the workforce, professional practice, and society; emerging opportunities and challenges; and the ethical implications of AI across fields.[^chunk:81794096-93b3-5e4c-81e8-eede3fdc05fc] Open to faculty, staff, students, regional leaders, government representatives, and the broader community, the Horizon Series fosters dialogue about what an AI-shaped future means for the University's graduates and the professions they will be entering.[^chunk:1db83593-eb35-5650-ad0d-fdf970607ec3]

### Faculty Innovation Grants

Scaled Faculty Innovation Grants support faculty-designed course and departmental projects.[^chunk:81794096-93b3-5e4c-81e8-eede3fdc05fc]

### Student Experience Navigator

The Student Experience Navigator program, an early version of which is already in pilot, provides a dedicated human guide who complements academic advising and helps students connect the parts of their Saint Joseph's University experience.[^chunk:81794096-93b3-5e4c-81e8-eede3fdc05fc]

### Human Formation Framework and Employee Development

The Initiative includes a framework to track and measure how core human formation competencies develop in Saint Joseph's students.[^chunk:81794096-93b3-5e4c-81e8-eede3fdc05fc] Additionally, development opportunities build employees' capacity to use emerging AI tools thoughtfully, effectively, and responsibly across the University.[^chunk:81794096-93b3-5e4c-81e8-eede3fdc05fc]

## Funding and Leadership

The Initiative includes strategic collaboration with and a lead gift from the Sanchez Dooner Foundation, whose leaders are Brian Dooner, BS '83 (president of the foundation) and Marlene Sanchez Dooner, BA '83.[^chunk:81794096-93b3-5e4c-81e8-eede3fdc05fc] Additional financial support comes from like-minded donors, with these gifts collectively representing a multi-million dollar investment that will support the University community for years to come.[^chunk:81794096-93b3-5e4c-81e8-eede3fdc05fc]

Jean McGivney-Burelle, PhD, Provost and Senior Vice President for Academic Affairs, emphasized the human-centered approach: "As the landscape of artificial intelligence continues to evolve, our focus must remain on people — creating opportunities for faculty, students and staff to engage these technologies thoughtfully, critically and ethically, while strengthening the distinctly human capacities that will matter even more in an age of AI."[^chunk:81794096-93b3-5e4c-81e8-eede3fdc05fc]
