# Association of Jesuit Colleges and Universities and Network Initiatives

The Association of Jesuit Colleges and Universities (AJCU) serves as the coordinating body for a comprehensive network of Jesuit-affiliated higher education institutions across North America. This section provides an overview of the AJCU's member institutions, collaborative initiatives, and efforts to deepen Jesuit and Catholic identity across the network.

## AJCU Member and Associate Institutions

The AJCU comprises a substantial network of institutions committed to Jesuit Catholic education. Member institutions include Boston College, Canisius University, College of the Holy Cross, Creighton University, Fairfield University, Fordham University, Georgetown University, Gonzaga University, John Carroll University, Le Moyne College, Loyola Marymount University, Loyola University Chicago, Loyola University Maryland, Loyola University New Orleans, Marquette University, Regis University, Rockhurst University, Saint Joseph's University, Saint Louis University, Saint Peter's University, Santa Clara University, Seattle University, Spring Hill College, The University of Detroit Mercy, The University of San Francisco, The University of Scranton, Xavier University, and St. John's College (Belize).[^chunk:eaf1eace-402b-509d-869f-6ac8e6891925] The association also includes associate members such as Campion College, Newman College, Regis College, and St. Mark's College.[^chunk:eaf1eace-402b-509d-869f-6ac8e6891925] In total, the AJCU encompasses 28 member institutions and 4 associate member institutions, bringing the complete network to 32 Jesuit-affiliated higher education institutions.[^chunk:eaf1eace-402b-509d-869f-6ac8e6891925]

## Deepening Jesuit and Catholic Identity at Member Institutions

Jesuit colleges demonstrate commitment to mission-focused collaboration through structured institutional processes. A key collaborative initiative is the Mission Priority Examen, a process required of all Jesuit colleges and universities to explore areas of strength and weakness in the life of an institution's mission.[^chunk:28ee9d8b-f65d-5276-83ba-a2e0ad82024b] The Examen is important for Jesuit colleges because it provides a structured opportunity for the entire campus community—faculty, staff, and students across all areas—to contribute their perspectives on institutional mission and identity.[^chunk:28ee9d8b-f65d-5276-83ba-a2e0ad82024b] Additionally, peer reviewers from other institutions evaluate the findings, bringing external perspective to the self-assessment process.[^chunk:28ee9d8b-f65d-5276-83ba-a2e0ad82024b]

During the 2018-19 academic year, Gonzaga University completed its Mission Priority Examen, representing a significant achievement in the university's approach to deepening its Jesuit, Catholic, and humanistic identity and practice.[^chunk:28ee9d8b-f65d-5276-83ba-a2e0ad82024b] The Examen process led to the identification of four institutional priorities: a Work Values project, a comprehensive Jesuit Identity Initiative, a reaffirmed mission-based commitment to Diversity, Equity and Cultural Fluency, and Administrative Restructuring to support the vitality and viability of the institution in service to its mission.[^chunk:28ee9d8b-f65d-5276-83ba-a2e0ad82024b]

## Grace Center for Faith and Community at Le Moyne College

The Grace Center for Faith and Community is a newly established center at Le Moyne College that was created during the 2025 spring semester.[^chunk:6ec9ec6f-f12f-5598-badc-1ecd56035dab] Located in the Panasci Family Chapel, the center encompasses mission integration, campus ministry, cultural and social engagement, faith formation and the Le Moyne Way.[^chunk:6ec9ec6f-f12f-5598-badc-1ecd56035dab]

The Grace Center represents Le Moyne's commitment to its Jesuit mission and social justice with a renewed organizational structure and personnel.[^chunk:6ec9ec6f-f12f-5598-badc-1ecd56035dab] According to President Linda LeMura, "The work of the Grace Center is a reflection of Le Moyne's unwavering commitment to educate and care for the whole person – mind, body and spirit – while cultivating a campus community rooted in reflection, purpose and a shared responsibility for one another."[^chunk:6ec9ec6f-f12f-5598-badc-1ecd56035dab] The center aims to raise the involvement of students, faculty, administrators, alumni and community partners to strengthen Le Moyne's identity as a Jesuit institution committed to service, faith and forming leaders who are compassionate, inclusive and grounded in social justice.[^chunk:6ec9ec6f-f12f-5598-badc-1ecd56035dab]

The Grace Center was created following a gift from Katherine "Kathy" Grace Purcell ʼ66 and her late husband John Purcell ʼ65.[^chunk:6ec9ec6f-f12f-5598-badc-1ecd56035dab] Kathy Purcell reflected on the center's establishment: "Knowing that the Grace Center will contribute so significantly to spirituality, faith formation and Le Moyne's Jesuit mission brings me great joy! Those were each a vital part of the experience that John and I valued during our time at Le Moyne."[^chunk:6ec9ec6f-f12f-5598-badc-1ecd56035dab]

## Scholarly Resources on Ignatian Pedagogy and Jesuit Education

Ignatian pedagogy is an educational philosophy rooted in the spiritual and intellectual tradition of St. Ignatius of Loyola, the founder of the Society of Jesus (Jesuits).[^chunk:62507f47-31f8-545a-9a08-516a164b9faa] This philosophy has distinguished Jesuit education for nearly 500 years and has made their order's educational institutions some of the most admired in the world.[^chunk:62507f47-31f8-545a-9a08-516a164b9faa]

At its core, Ignatian pedagogy is about "humanization," or forming students—not just teaching them—to be whole persons who live their lives for a greater good.[^chunk:30ded088-d2da-59bd-b8d6-5897bbe7fce7] Education in the Ignatian tradition awakens students to the dignity of themselves and others, leading them to discover their truest selves.[^chunk:30ded088-d2da-59bd-b8d6-5897bbe7fce7] At its best, this pedagogy forms students to recognize both flourishing and suffering in the world, enabling them to become agents of justice, humanization, and flourishing.[^chunk:30ded088-d2da-59bd-b8d6-5897bbe7fce7]

Ignatian pedagogy is based on St. Ignatius's method of helping individuals become closer to Jesus through imaginative prayer, reflection, and discernment—a critical, internal consideration of a situation or question and how it relates to one's personal or professional vocation.[^chunk:62507f47-31f8-545a-9a08-516a164b9faa]

## Ignatian Formation and Teaching Resources Across the Network

The Ignatian Pedagogical Paradigm encompasses several interconnected elements that work together to form whole persons. **Context** involves understanding who we are, where we come from, who others are, and why things are the way they are, including the impact of social systems and scientific paradigms.[^chunk:30ded088-d2da-59bd-b8d6-5897bbe7fce7]

**Experience** is holistic learning that honors both intellectual and affective dimensions. Affect and emotion are vital to the Ignatian paradigm, reflecting Ignatius's own emphasis on "sentir" or "felt knowledge."[^chunk:30ded088-d2da-59bd-b8d6-5897bbe7fce7]

**Reflection** involves finding meaning through critical engagement with lived experience, a cornerstone of Ignatian spirituality and pedagogy.[^chunk:30ded088-d2da-59bd-b8d6-5897bbe7fce7]

The three foundational commitments at Jesuit universities—competence, conscience, and compassion—are not arbitrary values but rather expressions of the institution's mission and identity rooted in the long tradition of Ignatian pedagogy.[^chunk:30ded088-d2da-59bd-b8d6-5897bbe7fce7] Jesuit institutions approach education through a holistic vision that rejects compartmentalization of knowledge.[^chunk:30ded088-d2da-59bd-b8d6-5897bbe7fce7]

## Jesuit Educational Mission and Identity Formation

Jesuit educational institutions are grounded in a distinctive pedagogical tradition centered on the concept of humanization.[^chunk:30ded088-d2da-59bd-b8d6-5897bbe7fce7] The Ignatian Pedagogical Paradigm emphasizes forming students as whole persons rather than simply transmitting knowledge, with the goal of developing leaders who will build a just, humane, and sustainable world.[^chunk:30ded088-d2da-59bd-b8d6-5897bbe7fce7]

The comprehensive core curriculum at Jesuit schools is not merely an add-on to professional education but rather a fundamental expression of the Ignatian vision of forming the whole person.[^chunk:30ded088-d2da-59bd-b8d6-5897bbe7fce7] This commitment explains pedagogical practices such as requiring artists to study science and chemists to study ethics, and ensuring all students engage questions of racial and gender justice.[^chunk:30ded088-d2da-59bd-b8d6-5897bbe7fce7]

Ignatian pedagogy is about accompanying students on a journey of learning, reflection, and self-discovery, fostering a deeper understanding of what it means to be human.[^chunk:30ded088-d2da-59bd-b8d6-5897bbe7fce7]
