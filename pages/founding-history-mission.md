# Founding History and Mission Identity

## Founding and Historical Development

Saint Joseph's University was founded in 1851 by the Jesuits[^chunk:be86e8d9-ccd0-5dea-b753-064eab7b4f0d], opening its doors with approximately 30 young men attending classes next to Old Saint Joseph's Church in Old City, Philadelphia[^chunk:be86e8d9-ccd0-5dea-b753-064eab7b4f0d]. The university has been challenging students to lead engaged lives for more than 170 years, steeping itself in a tradition of academic rigor and social justice[^chunk:be86e8d9-ccd0-5dea-b753-064eab7b4f0d].

The institution has undergone significant physical and institutional expansion throughout its history. In 1927, Saint Joseph's moved to Hawk Hill in Philadelphia and opened the first building on the City Avenue campus[^chunk:be86e8d9-ccd0-5dea-b753-064eab7b4f0d]. The university welcomed full-time female students in 1970[^chunk:be86e8d9-ccd0-5dea-b753-064eab7b4f0d]. More recently, in 2008, the 38-acre James J. Maguire '58 campus was added to Saint Joseph's in the suburbs on the Main Line[^chunk:be86e8d9-ccd0-5dea-b753-064eab7b4f0d]. In 2010, ground broke on the John R. Post '60 Academic Center and the John and Maryanne Hennings Post Learning Commons, which brought new, collaborative learning environments and a three-story addition to the Drexel Library[^chunk:be86e8d9-ccd0-5dea-b753-064eab7b4f0d].

The university received significant philanthropic support in 2017 when it announced receipt of a $50 million gift from James J. Maguire '58 and his wife, Frances—the single largest gift in the university's history[^chunk:be86e8d9-ccd0-5dea-b753-064eab7b4f0d]. In 2022, Saint Joseph's merged with University of the Sciences and expanded to a new University City location[^chunk:be86e8d9-ccd0-5dea-b753-064eab7b4f0d]. Most recently, in 2024, Saint Joseph's merged with Pennsylvania College of Health Sciences to provide in-demand program offerings in nursing and allied health[^chunk:be86e8d9-ccd0-5dea-b753-064eab7b4f0d].

A notable moment in the university's recent history occurred in 2015, when Pope Francis made a surprise trip to Hawk Hill, blessing the Synagoga and Ecclesia in Our Time sculpture and greeting community members[^chunk:be86e8d9-ccd0-5dea-b753-064eab7b4f0d].

## Mission Statement and Evolution

As Philadelphia's Jesuit Catholic University, Saint Joseph's provides a rigorous, student-centered education rooted in the liberal arts[^chunk:be86e8d9-ccd0-5dea-b753-064eab7b4f0d]. The university prepares students for personal excellence, professional success and engaged citizenship[^chunk:be86e8d9-ccd0-5dea-b753-064eab7b4f0d]. Striving to be an inclusive and diverse community that educates and cares for the whole person, Saint Joseph's encourages and models lifelong commitment to thinking critically, making ethical decisions, pursuing social justice and finding God in all things[^chunk:be86e8d9-ccd0-5dea-b753-064eab7b4f0d].

Today, the university focuses on preparing students to lead successful lives of meaning and impact in a complex, fast-changing world while honoring its past[^chunk:be86e8d9-ccd0-5dea-b753-064eab7b4f0d].

## Connection to Jesuit and Ignatian Traditions

Saint Joseph's University is one of only 27 Jesuit colleges and universities in the United States[^chunk:be86e8d9-ccd0-5dea-b753-064eab7b4f0d]. Throughout the world, the Jesuits are known for their achievements in education, service and the sciences, as well as their revolutionary spirit[^chunk:be86e8d9-ccd0-5dea-b753-064eab7b4f0d]. The order has started schools and colleges throughout the world, in many cases bringing educational opportunity to urban areas[^chunk:be86e8d9-ccd0-5dea-b753-064eab7b4f0d].

Saint Joseph's University embraces the Jesuit educational model, which recognizes that individuals are multidimensional—not just students—who need the freedom and encouragement to grow mentally, spiritually, personally and creatively[^chunk:be86e8d9-ccd0-5dea-b753-064eab7b4f0d]. This approach invites students to be both reflective and active—in the classroom and in the community[^chunk:be86e8d9-ccd0-5dea-b753-064eab7b4f0d].

The university is guided by the principles of Jesuit education, a holistic approach to learning that integrates intellectual development with moral and spiritual growth[^chunk:635ff496-128f-5afb-9a87-47e24e515de8]. Rooted in the teachings of St. Ignatius of Loyola[^chunk:635ff496-128f-5afb-9a87-47e24e515de8], Jesuit education seeks to form individuals who are committed to knowledge, faith, justice, and service[^chunk:635ff496-128f-5afb-9a87-47e24e515de8]. Ignatian education emphasizes:

- **Faith and Reflection**: Encouraging critical thinking in pursuit of truth[^chunk:635ff496-128f-5afb-9a87-47e24e515de8]
- **Service and Social Justice**: Working toward a just and equitable world[^chunk:635ff496-128f-5afb-9a87-47e24e515de8]
- **Cura Personalis**: Caring for each individual's intellectual, moral, and spiritual development[^chunk:635ff496-128f-5afb-9a87-47e24e515de8]
- **Magis**: Striving for excellence in all that we do[^chunk:635ff496-128f-5afb-9a87-47e24e515de8]

True to its Jesuit Catholic tradition, the university goes deeper at Saint Joseph's—deeper into self-reflection and critical thinking, so that the community can be of greater service for the world and for its people[^chunk:be86e8d9-ccd0-5dea-b753-064eab7b4f0d].

## The Office of Mission

The Office of Mission plays a central role in supporting the university's mission identity. Daniel R. J. Joyce, S.J. '88 serves as Vice President for Mission[^chunk:be86e8d9-ccd0-5dea-b753-064eab7b4f0d]. As a Jesuit university, Saint Joseph's is committed to educating and caring for the whole person across all aspects of identity[^chunk:be86e8d9-ccd0-5dea-b753-064eab7b4f0d]. The university strives to be a community of unique individuals, sharing a deep sense of belonging—where voices are heard, opinions are respected, and differences are celebrated and valued[^chunk:be86e8d9-ccd0-5dea-b753-064eab7b4f0d].

The Office of Mission provides resources and guidance for integrating the university's Ignatian mission into academic and community life. Faculty are provided with resources and discussion prompts to incorporate Jesuit values into classroom instruction, and the university celebrates Mission Week annually in February to reinforce these principles[^chunk:635ff496-128f-5afb-9a87-47e24e515de8].
