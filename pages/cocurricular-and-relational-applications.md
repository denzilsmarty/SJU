# Co-Curricular Applications: Advising, Ministry, Athletics, and Career Development

The Ignatian Pedagogical Paradigm (IPP) — with its five dynamics of context, experience, reflection, action, and evaluation — is not confined to the classroom. The 1993 source text *Ignatian Pedagogy: A Practical Approach* expressly extends the paradigm to academic and non-academic experiences alike, and Saint Joseph's University's own working framework explicitly names advising, community engagement, campus ministry, athletics, residence life, student leadership, and career development as settings where the IPP can coordinate formation without subordinating any one domain to another.[^chunk:a6c62be7-4932-5dfc-b4ea-52911d9de940]

This article explains how each of those settings employs the five dynamics, how *cura personalis* operates as an enabling condition across all of them, and where the distinctive guardrails of non-classroom use apply.

---

## Why the IPP Extends Beyond the Classroom

The IPP source text states that the paradigm can structure academic and non-academic experiences and that it applies in schools, colleges, universities, and other educational services.[^chunk:9a34ed4d-6e02-5417-886f-cc974e519300] This is consistent with the paradigm's architectural logic: any setting in which a person encounters reality, makes meaning, exercises freedom, acts responsibly, and learns from consequences is a site where the cycle can operate.[^chunk:9a34ed4d-6e02-5417-886f-cc974e519300]

The paradigm is also explicitly scalable. A cycle may operate within a class meeting, a course, a clinical rotation, a program, an advising relationship, or an institutional decision.[^chunk:9a34ed4d-6e02-5417-886f-cc974e519300] Co-curricular contexts typically run shorter, less formally structured cycles — a coaching conversation, a retreat day, a career-exploration exercise — but the underlying recursive logic of context → experience → reflection → action → evaluation remains the same.

SJU's institutional standard further calls for **integration**: core, professional, co-curricular, and relational learning should reinforce rather than fragment one another.[^chunk:514c92fa-878f-5bb4-b7dd-a668e30ffd19]

## Cura Personalis as the Relational Foundation

Across every co-curricular application, *cura personalis* functions as the essential enabling condition rather than as an additional step in the sequence. It requires knowledge of the learner, mutual trust, appropriate challenge, and adaptation to the person's actual situation and needs.[^chunk:9a34ed4d-6e02-5417-886f-cc974e519300] Without it, the five dynamics become a procedural checklist rather than an accompanied formative process.

*Cura personalis* is not simply "being nice." It joins personal knowledge and support to high expectations, and it attends to persons within the communities and structures that shape them.[^chunk:514c92fa-878f-5bb4-b7dd-a668e30ffd19]

## Advising and Mentoring

Academic and professional advising is one of the most naturally IPP-structured relationships on campus. The SJU framework describes advising as a process that:

- begins with the student's **context** — history, goals, constraints, and evolving identity;
- creates **experiences** of exploration (e.g., reviewing options, visiting offices, shadowing professionals);
- invites **reflection** on patterns and priorities;
- supports **informed choice** (action); and
- **revisits outcomes** at subsequent meetings (evaluation), which becomes the context for the next conversation.[^chunk:a6c62be7-4932-5dfc-b4ea-52911d9de940]

Good advising neither dictates vocation nor merely processes transactions.[^chunk:a6c62be7-4932-5dfc-b4ea-52911d9de940] This distinguishes IPP-informed advising from purely administrative scheduling: the advisor is an accompanying figure who holds both challenge and care.

AI tools may assist scheduling, information retrieval, and option comparison, but consequential conversations require identifiable human responsibility and escalation pathways.[^chunk:a6c62be7-4932-5dfc-b4ea-52911d9de940] This is consistent with the broader IPP principle that AI cannot assume the mutual obligation and moral accountability at the heart of *cura personalis*.[^chunk:a6c62be7-4932-5dfc-b4ea-52911d9de940]

## Community Engagement and Service

SJU defines communities as collaborators and co-educators, emphasizing reciprocal relationships and learning with and from partners — a posture described as strongly consonant with the IPP.[^chunk:a6c62be7-4932-5dfc-b4ea-52911d9de940]

The five dynamics apply as follows in community-engaged settings:

| Dynamic | Application in Community Engagement |
|---|---|
| **Context** | History, partner priorities, power, and the student's role |
| **Experience** | Direct immersion designed with — not merely for — community partners |
| **Reflection** | Examining both dignity and injustice encountered in the experience |
| **Action** | Reciprocal, co-designed responses rather than charity delivered to passive recipients |
| **Evaluation** | Partner voice, continuity, burden, and actual benefit — not only student growth |

The framework is explicit that contact with suffering is not a commodity for student growth, and that community partners must not be treated as instructional objects.[^chunk:a6c62be7-4932-5dfc-b4ea-52911d9de940]

## Campus Ministry and Retreats

Campus ministry can draw more explicitly from the Spiritual Exercises than any other co-curricular domain. In this setting, discernment, the *examen*, accompaniment, and habits of reflective action can be explored with directness unavailable in academic courses.[^chunk:a6c62be7-4932-5dfc-b4ea-52911d9de940]

Critical guardrails apply:

- Participation, personal disclosure, and sacramental practice must remain **free** and uncoerced.[^chunk:a6c62be7-4932-5dfc-b4ea-52911d9de940]
- Campus ministry supports the wider university but operates with **distinct authority and purposes** from academic units.[^chunk:a6c62be7-4932-5dfc-b4ea-52911d9de940]
- No student should be required to simulate belief or disclose private spiritual experience in any setting.[^chunk:9a34ed4d-6e02-5417-886f-cc974e519300]

These guardrails mirror the academic context: the educator (or minister) may disclose commitments and invite ethical and spiritual examination, but may not manufacture assent.[^chunk:9a34ed4d-6e02-5417-886f-cc974e519300]

## Athletics, Residence Life, and Student Leadership

Competition, teamwork, conflict, embodiment, belonging, and leadership all generate formative experience in the IPP sense — that is, direct encounter with reality that can be appropriated cognitively and affectively.[^chunk:a6c62be7-4932-5dfc-b4ea-52911d9de940] The IPP cycle applies directly:

- **Context**: team culture, competitive stakes, individual histories, power dynamics among coaches and players.
- **Experience**: performance, conflict, failure, and collaboration under pressure.
- **Reflection**: structured review that surfaces motivation, power, failure, resilience, dignity, and responsibility.
- **Action**: revised individual or team behavior, commitment, or leadership choice.
- **Evaluation**: includes climate, safety, inclusion, and conduct as well as performance outcomes.[^chunk:a6c62be7-4932-5dfc-b4ea-52911d9de940]

A key guardrail: "character formation" language cannot excuse coercion, humiliation, overwork, or preventable injury.[^chunk:a6c62be7-4932-5dfc-b4ea-52911d9de940] The same freedom and dignity standards that protect students in classrooms apply on courts, fields, and in residence halls.

## Career and Professional Development

Career education is perhaps the clearest demonstration that the IPP does not reduce to service or spiritual formation alone. The SJU framework describes career education as an opportunity to join *utilitas* (practical usefulness) to *humanitas* (full human development), *iustitia* (justice and the common good), and *fides* (ultimate meaning and vocation).[^chunk:a6c62be7-4932-5dfc-b4ea-52911d9de940] These four purposes — associated with the Ledesma–Kolvenbach account of higher education — give career development a horizon far wider than employability.[^chunk:9a34ed4d-6e02-5417-886f-cc974e519300]

The five dynamics in career development:

- **Context**: the student's talents, desires, social location, financial constraints, and the labor market.
- **Experience**: workplace visits, internships, informational interviews, and professional norms investigation.
- **Reflection**: examining how talents and desires align with contribution to the common good; noticing motivations for particular choices.
- **Action**: an informed career choice, a networking decision, or a vocational commitment.
- **Evaluation**: reflection on the effects of work on self, family, society, and creation.[^chunk:a6c62be7-4932-5dfc-b4ea-52911d9de940]

Employability and vocation should be integrated **without** claiming that every job choice has one spiritually correct answer.[^chunk:a6c62be7-4932-5dfc-b4ea-52911d9de940] The IPP protects freedom of conscience in career decisions just as in academic ones.

## Are the Five Dynamics Used the Same Way in Co-Curricular Contexts?

The five dynamics are the same; their **expression** differs by context. Several characteristics distinguish co-curricular application:

1. **Cycles are typically shorter and less formally structured.** A coaching conversation or advising session may move through all five dynamics in thirty minutes rather than across a semester.
2. **Reflection is less likely to be written or graded.** It may be oral, dialogic, or embodied. Personal appropriation matters; disclosure is not required.[^chunk:514c92fa-878f-5bb4-b7dd-a668e30ffd19]
3. **Action may be more immediate.** An athlete who reflects after a competition can change behavior in the next practice rather than waiting for an end-of-semester assignment.
4. **Evaluation must not become moral surveillance.** The same principle that prohibits grading a student's faith or political conformity in a course applies in residence life, athletics, and ministry: observable qualities of attention, reasoning, and conduct may be assessed, but interior moral worth may not be scored.[^chunk:9a34ed4d-6e02-5417-886f-cc974e519300]
5. **Cura personalis carries more of the structural load.** Without course syllabi and formal learning outcomes, the relational commitment of the advisor, minister, coach, or career counselor is the primary context-setting mechanism.[^chunk:a6c62be7-4932-5dfc-b4ea-52911d9de940]

The SJU design canvas confirms that the same five questions — governing purpose, context, experience, reflection, action, and evaluation — apply to "a class, course, program, advising process, co-curricular experience, policy, or institutional initiative."[^chunk:514c92fa-878f-5bb4-b7dd-a668e30ffd19]

## Connection to the Universal Apostolic Preferences

The Universal Apostolic Preferences (2019–2029) sharpen the contexts, experiences, and actions to which Jesuit education must attend without altering the five dynamics themselves.[^chunk:9a34ed4d-6e02-5417-886f-cc974e519300] Their call to walk with the excluded and journey with young people in creating a hope-filled future gives co-curricular work — especially community engagement, campus ministry, and student leadership — a specific horizon of responsibility.[^chunk:9a34ed4d-6e02-5417-886f-cc974e519300]

## Boundaries and Guardrails Across All Co-Curricular Settings

Several guardrails apply across every domain:

- **Distinguish roles clearly.** Teaching, advising, counseling, spiritual accompaniment, and clinical care have different authorities and scopes. Educators and staff must know when to refer students to qualified support.[^chunk:9a34ed4d-6e02-5417-886f-cc974e519300]
- **Never require trauma disclosure.** Reflective education can cross harmful lines if it confuses depth with personal confession. Alternative prompts that preserve the learning objective must always be available.[^chunk:a6c62be7-4932-5dfc-b4ea-52911d9de940]
- **Protect freedom of conscience.** People of every faith and of no religious affiliation are participants, not mission objects.[^chunk:9a34ed4d-6e02-5417-886f-cc974e519300]
- **Maintain accessibility.** The IPP requires attention to disability, race, gender, economic condition, culture, language, and other dimensions that shape access and experience in co-curricular settings just as in classrooms.[^chunk:a6c62be7-4932-5dfc-b4ea-52911d9de940]
- **AI tools assist but do not replace human accompaniment.** Systems can help identify patterns and offer information, but they cannot bear care, mutual obligation, fiduciary responsibility, or moral accountability.[^chunk:a6c62be7-4932-5dfc-b4ea-52911d9de940]
