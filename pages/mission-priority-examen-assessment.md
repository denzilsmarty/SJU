# Mission Priority Examen and Institutional Assessment Processes

## Overview

The Mission Priority Examen (MPE) and institutional assessment processes represent the integrated approach through which Jesuit colleges and universities in the United States, Belize, and Canada undertake systematic reflection on their Catholic and Jesuit identity, establish mission-driven priorities, and demonstrate institutional effectiveness.[^chunk:1b8e170f-7c0a-503a-9b71-3cba6453303e] Established at the request of the Jesuit Superior General by the North American Provincials and the Association of Jesuit Colleges and Universities (AJCU) presidents, and launched in 2016, the MPE integrates the centuries-old Ignatian practice of the Examen with structured cycles of self-study, peer review, and response from Rome.[^chunk:1b8e170f-7c0a-503a-9b71-3cba6453303e] This comprehensive wiki section addresses how Jesuit institutions structure governance, engage stakeholders, implement procedural steps, connect to regional accreditation, and translate mission priorities into concrete institutional action.

## The Mission Priority Examen in Context

The MPE serves dual institutional and ecclesial purposes. Toward the Society of Jesus, it connects the international Jesuit leadership with its higher-education ministries in North America, informs sponsorship decisions, and enables the Superior General to confirm each institution's Catholic and Jesuit identity publicly.[^chunk:1b8e170f-7c0a-503a-9b71-3cba6453303e] Toward the schools themselves, the process structures deep reflection on identity, produces explicit mission priorities for future development, and strengthens the network among Jesuit institutions.[^chunk:1b8e170f-7c0a-503a-9b71-3cba6453303e]

Crucially, the MPE is both evaluative and developmental—an honest look at institutional life ordered to growth, not an audit ordered to compliance.[^chunk:1b8e170f-7c0a-503a-9b71-3cba6453303e] While it bears surface resemblance to accreditation, the AJCU explicitly distinguishes the MPE as a collaborative, Ignatian experience among the campus community, peer institutions, the sponsoring Province, and the association, undertaken for the purpose of mission.[^chunk:1b8e170f-7c0a-503a-9b71-3cba6453303e]
