The **Ignatian Pedagogical Paradigm** (IPP) is the contemporary Society of Jesus's most widely used articulation of how educators can translate Ignatian spirituality and the Jesuit educational tradition into the concrete work of teaching and learning.[^chunk:9a34ed4d-6e02-5417-886f-cc974e519300] Its five named dynamics are **context**, **experience**, **reflection**, **action**, and **evaluation**.[^chunk:9a34ed4d-6e02-5417-886f-cc974e519300] Promulgated in 1993 by the International Commission on the Apostolate of Jesuit Education (ICAJE) under Superior General Peter-Hans Kolvenbach, S.J., the paradigm was written as a companion to *The Characteristics of Jesuit Education* (1986), responding to educators who wanted a usable way to bring that vision into ordinary teaching.[^chunk:9a34ed4d-6e02-5417-886f-cc974e519300]

This article explains what the IPP is, how it differs from conventional instructional models, what *cura personalis* means in practice, how the Four Cs relate to learning outcomes, and why the paradigm describes itself as recursive and relational rather than linear.

## 1. What the IPP Is—and Is Not

### A Flexible, Recursive Pattern

The IPP is not a five-step lesson template, a branded version of experiential learning, a requirement that every class include service, or a technique for leading students to predetermined conclusions.[^chunk:9a34ed4d-6e02-5417-886f-cc974e519300] The source document explicitly rejects the possibility of a single Jesuit curriculum for every country and discipline; it offers instead a flexible pattern that can be adapted to different cultures, institutions, subjects, learners, and historical circumstances.[^chunk:9a34ed4d-6e02-5417-886f-cc974e519300]

The clearest working formulation describes the IPP as:

> *A recursive, relational process through which educators and learners attend to context, engage reality through intellectually and affectively significant experience, reflect to discern meaning, choose and enact responsible responses, and evaluate both learning and its consequences so that the next cycle begins with greater freedom, competence, and care.*[^chunk:9a34ed4d-6e02-5417-886f-cc974e519300]

### Unpacking the Three-Part Name

**Ignatian** indicates family resemblance to the way Ignatius accompanies a person through the *Spiritual Exercises*: begin with the person's actual situation; invite an encounter; engage intellect, imagination, memory, affect, and will; discern movements and meanings; protect freedom; make a choice; and review what follows. The IPP does not turn the classroom into a retreat and does not make the professor a spiritual director; it translates an underlying way of proceeding into educational practice.[^chunk:9a34ed4d-6e02-5417-886f-cc974e519300]

**Pedagogical** means more than a collection of methods. The 1993 document defines pedagogy as the way teachers accompany learners in growth and development, which includes the educator's view of the person, the purpose of education, the teacher–learner relationship, the organization of knowledge, and the habits of inquiry and judgment that a course cultivates.[^chunk:9a34ed4d-6e02-5417-886f-cc974e519300]

**Paradigm** means a recurring pattern that orders judgment without prescribing a single sequence. New context can change the meaning of an experience; reflection can reveal that more experience is needed; evaluation can become experience for a new cycle; action can expose assumptions that require reconsideration.[^chunk:9a34ed4d-6e02-5417-886f-cc974e519300]

### What It Is Not

The IPP is not a five-box checklist, a compulsory linear sequence, a substitute for disciplinary expertise, a religious compliance test, a synonym for service-learning, a guarantee of justice or transformation, a technique for eliciting personal disclosure, a therapeutic model, a brand claim, or a finished theory with a large causal evidence base.[^chunk:9a34ed4d-6e02-5417-886f-cc974e519300] Its credibility depends on enacted practice and institutional alignment, and it is a normative and practical framework supported by historical continuity, accumulated practice, and a growing but still limited research literature.[^chunk:9a34ed4d-6e02-5417-886f-cc974e519300]

## 2. How the IPP Differs from Conventional Instructional Models

Conventional instructional models often treat teaching as the transmission of content and learning as the reliable reception of that content. The IPP differs in several fundamental ways.

### The Whole Person, Not Only the Intellect

The IPP assumes that a learner is more than a processor of information. Learning involves intellect, memory, imagination, emotion, embodiment, relationships, social location, desire, and will.[^chunk:9a34ed4d-6e02-5417-886f-cc974e519300] Education is formative because all learning changes what learners can see, value, and do; it becomes indoctrination when the educator suppresses inquiry, conceals contested premises, exploits authority, or treats predetermined agreement as evidence of learning.[^chunk:9a34ed4d-6e02-5417-886f-cc974e519300]

### Formation as Horizon

Formation is the overarching, lifelong purpose that the IPP serves—the intentional, accompanied, and lifelong integration of knowledge, experience, reflection, relationships, faith or ultimate meaning, and responsible action.[^chunk:9a34ed4d-6e02-5417-886f-cc974e519300] The IPP contributes to formation when repeated educational cycles become habits: attending carefully, entering experience, reflecting deeply, choosing responsibly, and learning from results.[^chunk:9a34ed4d-6e02-5417-886f-cc974e519300] Formation is not a single program or graduation outcome; it is not completed at graduation.[^chunk:9a34ed4d-6e02-5417-886f-cc974e519300]

### Convergences with—and Distinctions from—Other Pedagogies

The IPP converges with several modern pedagogies but should not be collapsed into any of them.[^chunk:6b47b6d8-cbae-5d9f-b6f7-2ec82ea750b0]

| Approach | Convergence | IPP Distinctive or Caution |
|---|---|---|
| Deweyan experiential education | Experience becomes educational through continuity, interaction, and reflection | IPP explicitly situates experience within discernment, formation, faith, justice, and responsible choice |
| Kolb's experiential learning cycle | Experience, reflection, conceptualization, experimentation recur | IPP gives context, affect, relationship, freedom, justice, and evaluation a wider role; it is not derived from Kolb |
| Schön's reflective practice | Professionals reflect in and on action amid uncertainty | IPP adds explicit attention to the whole person, social context, values, and formative purpose |
| Transformative learning | Critical reflection can revise frames of reference | IPP avoids equating transformation with dramatic change and emphasizes accompaniment and freedom |
| Backward design and authentic assessment | Purpose, evidence, and learning activities should align | IPP adds a recursive rather than only linear logic and asks who learners become through the design |

[^chunk:6b47b6d8-cbae-5d9f-b6f7-2ec82ea750b0]

The practical conclusion is eclecticism with criteria: the IPP should guide *why* and toward *what ends* methods are selected; learning science and disciplinary research should guide how reliably they work.[^chunk:6b47b6d8-cbae-5d9f-b6f7-2ec82ea750b0]

## 3. The Five Dynamics

The five dynamics should be visualized as a **spiral rather than a line**. Context surrounds the learning encounter. Experience, reflection, and action form its central movement. Evaluation judges learning and consequences and returns the learner and educator to a newly understood context.[^chunk:9a34ed4d-6e02-5417-886f-cc974e519300]

### Context

Context includes the learner's prior knowledge, experiences, feelings, values, readiness, social relationships, culture, and material conditions. It also includes the institutional setting and wider economic, political, religious, technological, and cultural environment. The 1993 text makes mutual trust and a personal teacher–student relationship central, linking context to *cura personalis*.[^chunk:9a34ed4d-6e02-5417-886f-cc974e519300] Context is not a demographic preface completed once at the start of a semester; it is an ongoing inquiry into what makes learning possible, difficult, meaningful, risky, or distorted.[^chunk:9a34ed4d-6e02-5417-886f-cc974e519300]

### Experience

Experience is any direct or vicarious encounter with the subject matter that the learner appropriates through the senses, intellect, imagination, memory, and affect. The IPP distinguishes cognitive grasp from affective response but insists that meaningful learning engages both.[^chunk:9a34ed4d-6e02-5417-886f-cc974e519300] Experience may be immediate—laboratory, clinical, community, studio, performance, field, or interpersonal—or mediated through texts, data, testimony, cases, images, simulations, and historical imagination.[^chunk:9a34ed4d-6e02-5417-886f-cc974e519300] The category is therefore wider than experiential education: a student's sustained encounter with a difficult theorem or poem can be an Ignatian experience if it is intellectually real, personally appropriated, and open to reflection.[^chunk:9a34ed4d-6e02-5417-886f-cc974e519300]

### Reflection

Reflection is the disciplined process by which learners grasp the significance of experience. It draws on memory, understanding, imagination, and feeling; examines causes, assumptions, motives, implications, alternatives, and consequences; and seeks greater freedom for choice. It is the **pivotal dynamic** connecting experience to responsible action.[^chunk:9a34ed4d-6e02-5417-886f-cc974e519300]

Ignatian reflection is not synonymous with journaling, introspection, or stating an opinion. It is epistemic, affective, ethical, social, and—when appropriate—spiritual. It asks both "What is true?" and "What is happening in me and among us as I encounter it?"[^chunk:9a34ed4d-6e02-5417-886f-cc974e519300] A disciplined sequence of questions moves through: attention (what happened?), interpretation (what patterns or causes explain it?), interior response (what draws or troubles me?), ethical and social meaning (who benefits, who bears risk?), and discernment (which response is more truthful and conducive to human flourishing?).[^chunk:9a34ed4d-6e02-5417-886f-cc974e519300]

### Action

In the IPP, action begins in interior appropriation—a judgment, choice, priority, value, or commitment that follows reflection—and may then become outward conduct. Reflection is incomplete if it produces understanding without considering response; action is irresponsible if it bypasses reflection.[^chunk:9a34ed4d-6e02-5417-886f-cc974e519300]

A clarified professional commitment, revised research design, refusal of an unethical practice, or plan for further inquiry can be genuine action. Visible activity without changed understanding may be pedagogically empty.[^chunk:9a34ed4d-6e02-5417-886f-cc974e519300]

### Evaluation

Evaluation provides feedback, uncovers blind spots, and begins a new cycle. It has at least four objects: (1) academic learning—what knowledge and skills have been demonstrated; (2) quality of judgment—how well evidence, values, perspectives, and consequences have been integrated; (3) action and impact—what was done and with what results; and (4) educational design—what the teacher, program, or institution should change.[^chunk:9a34ed4d-6e02-5417-886f-cc974e519300]

## 4. Why the IPP Is Recursive and Relational Rather Than Linear

The five dynamics are distinguishable, but real learning moves among them in a spiral.[^chunk:9a34ed4d-6e02-5417-886f-cc974e519300] A cycle may operate within a single class meeting, a course, a clinical rotation, a program, an advising relationship, or an institutional decision.[^chunk:9a34ed4d-6e02-5417-886f-cc974e519300] New context can change the meaning of an experience; reflection can reveal that more experience is needed; evaluation can become the experience that opens a new cycle.[^chunk:9a34ed4d-6e02-5417-886f-cc974e519300]

The relational character stems from the Ignatian tradition of *accompaniment*—walking with another person in a relationship that combines presence, listening, challenge, and respect for freedom.[^chunk:514c92fa-878f-5bb4-b7dd-a668e30ffd19] The teacher does not stand between the learner and reality as its sole interpreter. The educator designs conditions in which the learner can encounter a subject, evidence, other persons, and consequences with increasing honesty and skill.[^chunk:9a34ed4d-6e02-5417-886f-cc974e519300] Authority is exercised through expertise, clarity, care, feedback, and appropriate challenge—not through manufactured assent.[^chunk:9a34ed4d-6e02-5417-886f-cc974e519300]

## 5. Cura Personalis in Practice

*Cura personalis* (Latin: care for the whole person) is a relational commitment and enabling condition of the IPP, not a sixth step and not simply "being nice."[^chunk:9a34ed4d-6e02-5417-886f-cc974e519300] It is best understood as care for the person in their concrete particularity and wholeness, expressed through knowledge, relationship, adaptation, support, challenge, and respect for freedom.[^chunk:514c92fa-878f-5bb4-b7dd-a668e30ffd19]

### What It Requires of Faculty

*Cura personalis* requires knowledge of learners, trust, appropriate challenge, and adaptation.[^chunk:9a34ed4d-6e02-5417-886f-cc974e519300] The 1993 text makes mutual trust and a personal teacher–student relationship central to the entire paradigm.[^chunk:9a34ed4d-6e02-5417-886f-cc974e519300] In practice this means:

- diagnosing what learners already know, misunderstand, fear, value, or hope[^chunk:9a34ed4d-6e02-5417-886f-cc974e519300]
- attending to linguistic, cultural, financial, disability-related, family, work, or caregiving conditions that affect participation[^chunk:9a34ed4d-6e02-5417-886f-cc974e519300]
- joining personal knowledge and support to high expectations (*care with challenge*)[^chunk:a6c62be7-4932-5dfc-b4ea-52911d9de940]
- providing feedback that begins a new cycle of growth[^chunk:9a34ed4d-6e02-5417-886f-cc974e519300]

### What It Is Not

*Cura personalis* is not individualized instruction in a narrow sense, though individualization may express it. It also attends to persons within communities and structures.[^chunk:514c92fa-878f-5bb4-b7dd-a668e30ffd19] It does not license pressing students to disclose trauma or private experience—context becomes voyeurism if students are so pressed.[^chunk:9a34ed4d-6e02-5417-886f-cc974e519300] The proper standard is *informed adaptation with high expectations and appropriate support*.[^chunk:9a34ed4d-6e02-5417-886f-cc974e519300]

### Boundaries with Other Roles

Educators must know when to refer students to qualified support. The IPP is not a therapeutic model, and the educator is not a spiritual director.[^chunk:9a34ed4d-6e02-5417-886f-cc974e519300] Reflective education can cross boundaries if it confuses depth with disclosure; SJU practice should distinguish academic reflection from therapy, confession, and spiritual direction, and never require disclosure of sensitive information unless essential, lawful, and appropriately protected.[^chunk:a6c62be7-4932-5dfc-b4ea-52911d9de940]

## 6. The Four Cs and Learning Outcomes

### What the Four Cs Are

ICAJE's 2015 document *Human Excellence* described the desired qualities of students in four terms: **conscience, competence, compassion, and commitment**.[^chunk:6b47b6d8-cbae-5d9f-b6f7-2ec82ea750b0] These are formative qualities—habits and orientations—not stages in a sequence.[^chunk:9a34ed4d-6e02-5417-886f-cc974e519300]

### Relationship to the IPP

The Four Cs name outcomes or habits toward which repeated IPP cycles may contribute over time.[^chunk:9a34ed4d-6e02-5417-886f-cc974e519300] They are the *direction* the spiral of context–experience–reflection–action–evaluation is meant to travel, not additional steps within it.[^chunk:9a34ed4d-6e02-5417-886f-cc974e519300] No single course or experience can claim to produce them; they are the cumulative result of formation across learning encounters.[^chunk:9a34ed4d-6e02-5417-886f-cc974e519300]

### Cautions for Learning Outcome Design

Institutional variants of the Four Cs should not be confused with the globally promulgated framework, and an unofficial fifth C should not be presented as universal.[^chunk:9a34ed4d-6e02-5417-886f-cc974e519300] More importantly, the IPP's formative ambition creates a serious risk: converting accompaniment into surveillance or assigning grades to interior life.[^chunk:9a34ed4d-6e02-5417-886f-cc974e519300]

Faculty may assess the quality of ethical analysis, reflective practice, professional responsibility, collaboration, perspective-taking, and evidence-based action when these are legitimate learning outcomes. They should not claim to measure the state of a student's conscience, compassion, faith, political virtue, or human worth.[^chunk:9a34ed4d-6e02-5417-886f-cc974e519300]

Assessable evidence includes the specificity of observation, use of relevant concepts, acknowledgment of uncertainty, examination of assumptions, engagement with affected perspectives, proportionality of response, revision after feedback, and analysis of consequences. Rubrics should describe observable qualities in work, not identity claims about the person.[^chunk:9a34ed4d-6e02-5417-886f-cc974e519300]

## 7. The IPP at Saint Joseph's University

For Saint Joseph's University, the IPP is proposed as an integrating architecture across the aims of academic excellence, experiential learning, belonging and human connection, and formation in Ignatian spirituality and pedagogy.[^chunk:a6c62be7-4932-5dfc-b4ea-52911d9de940] It does not replace the General Education Program, Cornerstone Core, professional accreditation, learning outcomes, or unit strategies; it gives them a common process language.[^chunk:a6c62be7-4932-5dfc-b4ea-52911d9de940]

The institution itself teaches. Policies, incentives, technologies, spaces, workloads, partnerships, and decision processes form people as surely as syllabi do; IPP-inspired institutional practice asks what SJU's arrangements cause people to experience, value, and do.[^chunk:9a34ed4d-6e02-5417-886f-cc974e519300]

The paradigm is also described as more—not less—important in an AI-shaped university. Generative systems can assist research, feedback, accessibility, and rehearsal, but they cannot assume responsibility, enter a mutual relationship, discern a vocation, or substitute for the human encounter at the heart of *cura personalis*.[^chunk:9a34ed4d-6e02-5417-886f-cc974e519300]

## 8. Evidence and Limitations

The IPP has strong documentary and practice-based legitimacy within Jesuit education; its empirical research base is more modest.[^chunk:a6c62be7-4932-5dfc-b4ea-52911d9de940] The literature generally reports feasibility, reflective depth, integration of values and disciplinary learning, or perceived formation; it less often uses comparative designs, longitudinal measures, independent outcomes, or large multi-institutional samples.[^chunk:a6c62be7-4932-5dfc-b4ea-52911d9de940]

DeFeo's 2009 study of U.S. Jesuit teaching and learning centers found broad familiarity and perceived viability but also uneven implementation, with action less prominent than context, experience, and reflection.[^chunk:a6c62be7-4932-5dfc-b4ea-52911d9de940] Mummadi's 2021 qualitative study in India emphasized the paradigm's potential for teacher identity and vocation while identifying the need for deeper social analysis, leadership support, and formation.[^chunk:a6c62be7-4932-5dfc-b4ea-52911d9de940]

Calibrated claims are therefore appropriate: what the IPP means and intends is well supported by primary sources; that structured experience, reflection, authentic action, feedback, relational teaching, and contextualized design can improve learning under appropriate conditions is supported by accumulated practice and related research; claims that a particular IPP implementation improves belonging, ethical reasoning, or community impact are promising but require local evidence; that labeling a course "IPP" causes formation is not established.[^chunk:a6c62be7-4932-5dfc-b4ea-52911d9de940]
