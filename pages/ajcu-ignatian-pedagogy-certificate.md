# AJCU Ignatian Pedagogy Certificate Program and Faculty Development

The AJCU Ignatian Pedagogy Certificate Program is a comprehensive faculty development initiative designed to deepen educators' understanding of Jesuit educational values and practices across the Association of Jesuit Colleges and Universities network. [^chunk:9b6c3a4b-e85a-57e2-a927-d33530e2bae4]

## Program Origins and Leadership

The certificate program was created in 2022 by Rev. Michael J. Garanzini, S.J., former President of AJCU, who established a commission composed of educators from Jesuit institutions "to breathe renewed life into our long-standing mission and its pedagogical implications." [^chunk:9b6c3a4b-e85a-57e2-a927-d33530e2bae4] [^chunk:daa87a54-04f4-54cd-a941-5d13c03b5895] This program emerged as the principal deliverable of a multi-year collaboration of representatives from across the AJCU network. [^chunk:9b6c3a4b-e85a-57e2-a927-d33530e2bae4]

## Program Structure

The AJCU Ignatian Pedagogy Certificate Program is structured around six core modules, with an optional seventh module. [^chunk:9b6c3a4b-e85a-57e2-a927-d33530e2bae4]

### Core Modules

**Module 1: Introduction to the IP Certificate Program** provides a brief overview of the principles inherent in Jesuit education and an introduction to the Ignatian Pedagogical Paradigm as "the foundational overlay that is at the heart of the Ignatian instructional approach." [^chunk:9b6c3a4b-e85a-57e2-a927-d33530e2bae4]

**Module 2: Foundations of Jesuit Education** explores the relationship between Ignatian pedagogy and the Spiritual Exercises of St. Ignatius of Loyola and provides an overview of concepts foundational to understanding Jesuit education. [^chunk:9b6c3a4b-e85a-57e2-a927-d33530e2bae4]

**Module 3: The Ignatian Pedagogical Paradigm (IPP)** examines the five domains of the Ignatian Pedagogical Paradigm and how the IPP impacts educational practice at Jesuit institutions. [^chunk:9b6c3a4b-e85a-57e2-a927-d33530e2bae4]

**Module 4: A Faith That Does Justice, Part I** provides a historical overview of the Jesuit commitment to justice, including various ways in which student learning can sustain the Jesuit educational commitment to solidarity in contemporary society. [^chunk:9b6c3a4b-e85a-57e2-a927-d33530e2bae4]

**Module 5: A Faith That Does Justice, Part II** explores the dynamics of Ignatian pedagogy in conversation with contemporary movements in critical, inclusive pedagogy and other pedagogical practices. [^chunk:9b6c3a4b-e85a-57e2-a927-d33530e2bae4]

**Module 6: Cura Personalis and Accompanying Students Toward a Hope-Filled Future** includes readings and reflections that help participants identify contemporary challenges confronting students and considerations for how educators can respond to them. [^chunk:9b6c3a4b-e85a-57e2-a927-d33530e2bae4]

### Optional Campus-Created Module

Following completion of the program's six modules, campuses are encouraged to develop a seventh module unique to their institution. [^chunk:9b6c3a4b-e85a-57e2-a927-d33530e2bae4] This module allows campuses to identify issues currently important to their faculty, staff, and students, select relevant readings and activities, and create discussion questions to facilitate engagement and reflection on campus-specific topics. [^chunk:9b6c3a4b-e85a-57e2-a927-d33530e2bae4]

## Competencies and Learning Outcomes

The certificate program is designed to help participants develop knowledge and competencies related to Ignatian pedagogy through engagement with rich and effective learning in dialogue with the Ignatian tradition, contemporary challenges, and pedagogical approaches. [^chunk:9b6c3a4b-e85a-57e2-a927-d33530e2bae4] The program aims to establish a cohort of Ignatian pedagogy practitioners active in all aspects of campus life. [^chunk:9b6c3a4b-e85a-57e2-a927-d33530e2bae4]

The program is intentionally structured as "alive and adaptable," recognizing that "educational practice is only effective insofar as the practitioners are willing to learn and grow alongside it." [^chunk:9b6c3a4b-e85a-57e2-a927-d33530e2bae4]

## Delivery Format and Credentials

The certificate program operates as a facilitated, modular program for campus cohorts of faculty and staff. [^chunk:daa87a54-04f4-54cd-a941-5d13c03b5895] Instructional materials are delivered through downloadable LMS (Learning Management System) cartridges available for multiple learning management systems, with module pages and cartridges being password-protected. [^chunk:daa87a54-04f4-54cd-a941-5d13c03b5895] Campuses must register their cohorts with the AJCU IP Certificate Coordinator at IPP@ajcunet.edu to receive access credentials. [^chunk:9b6c3a4b-e85a-57e2-a927-d33530e2bae4]

Participants who successfully complete all six modules receive a certificate signed by the president of AJCU. [^chunk:9b6c3a4b-e85a-57e2-a927-d33530e2bae4]

## Campus Integration and Implementation

Several AJCU member institutions have integrated the certificate program into their faculty development offerings. Marquette University's Center for Teaching and Learning partnered with Mission & Ministry to offer six-session in-person cohorts with 90-minute sessions, recruiting a Fall 2026 cohort. [^chunk:daa87a54-04f4-54cd-a941-5d13c03b5895] Regis University implemented the certificate program as a Spring 2025 learning community organized into six biweekly sessions. [^chunk:daa87a54-04f4-54cd-a941-5d13c03b5895]

## Philosophy and Purpose

The program explicitly aims to help build a community of knowledgeable practitioners on campuses who understand the values inherent in Jesuit institutions. [^chunk:9b6c3a4b-e85a-57e2-a927-d33530e2bae4] By encouraging the creation of campus-specific seventh modules, the program promotes active engagement with locally relevant issues while maintaining connection to broader Ignatian and Jesuit educational principles. [^chunk:9b6c3a4b-e85a-57e2-a927-d33530e2bae4]
