# Case Study: Marquette University Mission Priority Examen

## Overview

Marquette University is participating in the Association of Jesuit Colleges and Universities (AJCU) Mission Priority Examen process during the 2025-26 academic year, with a site visit scheduled for 2027.[^chunk:3c27ec5e-edbb-5a9d-9a1d-0649176d682b] The Mission Priority Examen provides space and structure for each Jesuit college and university to undertake serious discussions about how they understand and live out their mission, establish goals for the future, and set forth new or renewed means of realizing those goals in the years to come.[^chunk:3c27ec5e-edbb-5a9d-9a1d-0649176d682b]

## Leadership and Key Personnel

President Kimo Ah Yun has named two co-chairs to lead Marquette's Mission Priority Examen efforts: Dr. Stephanie Quade, retired dean of students, and Bill Fliss, manuscripts archivist in Raynor Library.[^chunk:3c27ec5e-edbb-5a9d-9a1d-0649176d682b] Quade and Fliss will partner with Rev. John Thiede, S.J., vice president for mission and ministry, to form a Mission Priority Examen steering committee.[^chunk:3c27ec5e-edbb-5a9d-9a1d-0649176d682b] The steering committee and eventual working groups will gather input from the campus community and other stakeholders during the process.

## Previous MPE Experience

Dr. Stephanie Quade co-chaired Marquette's last Mission Priority Examen during the 2019-20 academic year, during which the university received a positive evaluation.[^chunk:3c27ec5e-edbb-5a9d-9a1d-0649176d682b] Following that process, Rev. Arturo Sosa, S.J., superior general of the Society of Jesus, called Marquette "one of the strongest universities in the Association of Jesuit Colleges and Universities network in the United States."[^chunk:3c27ec5e-edbb-5a9d-9a1d-0649176d682b]

## Timeline for 2025-26 MPE Process

The Marquette Mission Priority Examen follows a structured timeline:[^chunk:3c27ec5e-edbb-5a9d-9a1d-0649176d682b]

- **Spring 2026**: The steering committee and working groups will gather input from the campus community and other stakeholders
- **Fall 2026**: The draft findings from the self-study will be completed
- **2027**: A team of peer visitors will come to campus to meet with key individuals, including President Ah Yun, leadership teams, and campus groups to gather their own feedback

## Reflection on AJCU Practices

Marquette's approach to the Mission Priority Examen reflects broader AJCU practices by participating in a structured, self-reflective process designed to help Jesuit institutions assess and renew their mission commitments.[^chunk:3c27ec5e-edbb-5a9d-9a1d-0649176d682b] President Ah Yun emphasized the importance of this work, stating: "Reflecting deeply on our Jesuit mission in the spirit of St. Ignatius is one of the most important exercises we as a university community can undertake."[^chunk:3c27ec5e-edbb-5a9d-9a1d-0649176d682b]
