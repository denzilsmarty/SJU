# Cura Personalis and Cura Apostolica: Ignatian Care Concepts

## Overview

Cura personalis (care of the individual person) and cura apostolica (apostolic care or care of the mission) represent two complementary dimensions of Jesuit life and governance rooted in the spiritual legacy of Ignatius of Loyola.[^chunk:6f4937fe-63ab-5d7e-b5d0-240d7e30ca51] These concepts reflect a tension proper to the charism and way of proceeding of the Society of Jesus, gaining particular importance in contemporary apostolic contexts characterized by cooperation with lay colleagues and new forms of governance.[^chunk:6f4937fe-63ab-5d7e-b5d0-240d7e30ca51]

## Historical Development of Cura Personalis

### Early Formulation

The expression *cura personalis* appears for the first time (or among the first times) in the instruction of Fr. Ledóchowski on universities and colleges in the Assistancy of the United States of America dated 15 August 1934.[^chunk:6d4cc816-54ef-55c9-b6c3-0d8535ff91a8] This instruction was subsequently revised by Fr. Janssens on 27 September 1948.[^chunk:6d4cc816-54ef-55c9-b6c3-0d8535ff91a8]

### Ignatian Roots

Although the formal terminology is relatively recent, the concept derives from lived experience deeply embedded in Ignatius of Loyola's spiritual journey. The expressions *cura apostolica* and *cura personalis* are relatively recent in the tradition of the Society, yet they refer to an experience that runs through the entire life of St. Ignatius, which he describes as "care."[^chunk:6f4937fe-63ab-5d7e-b5d0-240d7e30ca51] Ignatius's Autobiography recounts his experience of being cared for by God and subsequently caring for others, while the Spiritual Exercises represent the mystical journey of God's care for the human vocation through Christ's incarnation, death, and resurrection.[^chunk:6f4937fe-63ab-5d7e-b5d0-240d7e30ca51] The Constitutions, particularly in parts IV, VIII, and IX, embody the commitment of those who govern to preserve and care for the universal Society in accord with its mission.[^chunk:6f4937fe-63ab-5d7e-b5d0-240d7e30ca51]

## Distinction and Complementarity

### Separate but Inseparable

Cura apostolica and cura personalis represent inseparable and complementary dimensions of life-mission in the Society.[^chunk:6f4937fe-63ab-5d7e-b5d0-240d7e30ca51] However, difficulties in Jesuit practice derive mainly from separating the two dimensions.[^chunk:6d4cc816-54ef-55c9-b6c3-0d8535ff91a8] When separation occurs and competences are divided juridically between the Local Superior and the Director of the Work, this division does not further the care of mission.[^chunk:6d4cc816-54ef-55c9-b6c3-0d8535ff91a8] Reducing the relationship between cura apostolica and cura personalis to its juridical limits and attempting to distribute responsibilities between the Superior and the Director of the Work destroy the necessary tension between them that makes possible the care of life-mission according to the Ignatian charism.[^chunk:6d4cc816-54ef-55c9-b6c3-0d8535ff91a8]

### Unity in Mission

Ignatius's experience demonstrates that care, deeply rooted in his spiritual experience and mystical journey, offers the true dimension of unity of cura apostolica-cura personalis as dimensions of one single cura—care for mission.[^chunk:6d4cc816-54ef-55c9-b6c3-0d8535ff91a8] This single cura has as its focus persons, communities, and works at the service of mission.[^chunk:6d4cc816-54ef-55c9-b6c3-0d8535ff91a8] It is mission that must be the fundamental criterion uniting cura apostolica and cura personalis.[^chunk:6d4cc816-54ef-55c9-b6c3-0d8535ff91a8]

The Society's mission includes and implies inseparably the way members live and relate to each other and care for people and communities.[^chunk:6d4cc816-54ef-55c9-b6c3-0d8535ff91a8] This care for one's way of living and relating is itself a mission.[^chunk:6d4cc816-54ef-55c9-b6c3-0d8535ff91a8]

### Fruitful Tension

Living the tension between cura apostolica and cura personalis in a healthy and constructive way is essential to genuine care of mission.[^chunk:6d4cc816-54ef-55c9-b6c3-0d8535ff91a8] The goal is not to eliminate this tension but to live it fruitfully through close cooperation between the Local Superior and the Director of the Work.[^chunk:6d4cc816-54ef-55c9-b6c3-0d8535ff91a8] Frequent formal conversations between these leaders foster care of the mission and strengthen the community's apostolic character.[^chunk:6d4cc816-54ef-55c9-b6c3-0d8535ff91a8]

## Contemporary Application in Jesuit Higher Education and Apostolic Works

### Responsibility Across the Apostolic Body

While the care of mission belongs to the whole body of the Society, this responsibility falls primarily within the competence of Major Superiors.[^chunk:6d4cc816-54ef-55c9-b6c3-0d8535ff91a8] However, Delegates, Local Superiors, members of the community, Directors of Work, and other Jesuits together with their companions in mission in the works should also assume care of mission according to their different levels of responsibility.[^chunk:6d4cc816-54ef-55c9-b6c3-0d8535ff91a8]

In the care of mission, the special nature of different vocations of those who participate must be taken into account: lay men and women, Jesuits, non-believers, other religious men and women, diocesan priests, and believers of various religions.[^chunk:6d4cc816-54ef-55c9-b6c3-0d8535ff91a8] The challenge for leaders is to create conditions so that all feel they can fully develop their capacities according to their personal characteristics, their vocation, and their level of responsibility in apostolic work.[^chunk:6d4cc816-54ef-55c9-b6c3-0d8535ff91a8]

### Spiritual Conversation and Discernment

Spiritual conversation and discernment in common constitute excellent instruments for care of mission in which all those involved in mission should participate.[^chunk:6d4cc816-54ef-55c9-b6c3-0d8535ff91a8] Spiritual conversation, as a habitual mode of exchange in communities and apostolic works, prepares for discernment in common as the ordinary way of reaching decisions in the life-mission of the Society.[^chunk:6d4cc816-54ef-55c9-b6c3-0d8535ff91a8]

Spiritual conversation presupposes attentive listening to oneself and to others and listening to the Spirit who speaks through shared experience, opening participants to new perspectives on reality.[^chunk:6d4cc816-54ef-55c9-b6c3-0d8535ff91a8] This practice strengthens mutual trust and leads to deeper self-knowledge, knowledge of others, and understanding of the context in which mission is lived out.[^chunk:6d4cc816-54ef-55c9-b6c3-0d8535ff91a8] It becomes preparation for discernment in common as a process for "seeking and finding" God's will.[^chunk:6d4cc816-54ef-55c9-b6c3-0d8535ff91a8]

### Role of Local Superiors

Local Superiors strengthen their confreres in apostolic mission by taking care that their way of life makes them fit to fulfill it.[^chunk:66689b9c-34a3-54b7-8f49-1734d388da5c] For this to be effective, Superiors should visit their members in their apostolic environment and converse with the Director of the Work about their activities, thereby contributing to making the community truly apostolic.[^chunk:66689b9c-34a3-54b7-8f49-1734d388da5c]

When the community is responsible for an apostolic work, it is the Local Superior's role to encourage Jesuit members of the apostolic team and, cooperating with the Director, to foster the Ignatian and Jesuit identity of the institution.[^chunk:66689b9c-34a3-54b7-8f49-1734d388da5c] Such cooperation requires personal conversion and self-awareness and demands strengthening skills and formation procedures aimed at shared leadership.[^chunk:66689b9c-34a3-54b7-8f49-1734d388da5c]

### Partnership and Shared Mission

Partnership and shared mission with lay colleagues and other partners reflect not a response to declining numbers of Jesuits but rather realization that by fostering conversation, consultation, and discernment in common, the apostolic body of the Society is being built up following the inspiration of the Holy Spirit through Vatican II.[^chunk:6d4cc816-54ef-55c9-b6c3-0d8535ff91a8]

The intimate relationship between life and mission extends beyond the religious community to those persons who, not being Jesuits, participate according to their vocation in the Society's mission.[^chunk:6d4cc816-54ef-55c9-b6c3-0d8535ff91a8] Both Jesuits and non-Jesuits are responsible for each other's vocations so that all vocations find their place and potential for development.[^chunk:6d4cc816-54ef-55c9-b6c3-0d8535ff91a8]

Caring for one another and being cared for frees the apostolic body from clericalism, paternalism, individualism, and authoritarianism found in many current contexts.[^chunk:6d4cc816-54ef-55c9-b6c3-0d8535ff91a8] This mutual care is an essential part of the culture of the apostolic body and represents an important dimension of life-mission for contemporary Jesuit institutions.[^chunk:6d4cc816-54ef-55c9-b6c3-0d8535ff91a8]

### Contemporary Challenges

Contemporary Jesuit higher education and apostolic works face complex governance structures that, while offering opportunities for greater integration between Superiors, Delegates, and Directors of Work, can jeopardize accompaniment of both curae if adequate strategies for delegating responsibilities are not employed.[^chunk:6f4937fe-63ab-5d7e-b5d0-240d7e30ca51] New structures of governance require careful attention to maintaining the role and effectiveness of the Local Superior and ensuring that apostolic concerns are discussed inclusively rather than directly between sectoral coordinators and Major Superiors, bypassing community leadership.[^chunk:6f4937fe-63ab-5d7e-b5d0-240d7e30ca51]

In multi-apostolic communities, Local Superiors sometimes find difficulty accompanying apostolic concerns or institutions whose structure or areas of activity are unfamiliar or where relationships with governing bodies are challenging.[^chunk:6f4937fe-63ab-5d7e-b5d0-240d7e30ca51] Additionally, there are situations where Directors of Work enjoy excessive autonomy with respect to local and provincial government, resulting in personal identification with their work and loss of freedom for apostolic spiritual discernment.[^chunk:6f4937fe-63ab-5d7e-b5d0-240d7e30ca51]
