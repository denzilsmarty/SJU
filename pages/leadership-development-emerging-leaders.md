# Leadership Development Programs for Emerging and Senior Leaders

The Association of Jesuit Colleges and Universities (AJCU) offers a comprehensive suite of leadership development programs designed to cultivate Ignatian leadership across different career stages and organizational levels within Jesuit higher education institutions.

## AJCU Leadership Development Pathways

AJCU provides four primary leadership development programs that form a progressive pathway for leaders at different levels of their careers and institutional roles.

### Ignatian Leadership Seminar (ILS)

The Ignatian Leadership Seminar serves as an introduction to foundational Ignatian leadership principles and development.[^chunk:f0216da4-c2cc-5ec1-91b3-73cd823aa8b4] This program targets emerging and newly appointed leaders, specifically new or early-career faculty and staff who have demonstrated interest in learning more about the Ignatian charism and AJCU network.[^chunk:f0216da4-c2cc-5ec1-91b3-73cd823aa8b4] The ILS format combines 10 monthly online sessions featuring topical presentations and small group discussions, concluding with a 4-day in-person seminar held in Chicago.[^chunk:f0216da4-c2cc-5ec1-91b3-73cd823aa8b4]

### Ignatian Colleagues Program (ICP)

The Ignatian Colleagues Program provides advanced, in-depth development of Ignatian discerning leadership for senior-level administrators and faculty.[^chunk:f0216da4-c2cc-5ec1-91b3-73cd823aa8b4] This program is designed for senior-level leaders whose roles influence a large sector of the university community and/or campus culture.[^chunk:f0216da4-c2cc-5ec1-91b3-73cd823aa8b4] The 18-month program integrates online modules, small-group dialogues, an in-person retreat, an international encounter experience, and a capstone project that benefits participants' home campuses.[^chunk:f0216da4-c2cc-5ec1-91b3-73cd823aa8b4] The ICP builds upon foundational knowledge from the ILS or similar programs.

### Ignatian Executive Leadership Academy (IELA)

The Ignatian Executive Leadership Academy focuses on executive-level leadership development for senior leaders in dean-level or higher supervisory positions.[^chunk:f0216da4-c2cc-5ec1-91b3-73cd823aa8b4] Participants must have a foundational understanding of the Jesuit and Catholic mission and be discerning their future role in Jesuit higher education.[^chunk:f0216da4-c2cc-5ec1-91b3-73cd823aa8b4] The program consists of three 4-day sessions conducted over six months, exposing participants to the diverse aspects, populations, and realities within Jesuit higher education.[^chunk:f0216da4-c2cc-5ec1-91b3-73cd823aa8b4]

### New Presidents Orientation

The New Presidents Orientation program welcomes new and recently appointed presidents into the AJCU Network and deepens their understanding of issues related to the Society of Jesus, the Catholic Church, and AJCU.[^chunk:f0216da4-c2cc-5ec1-91b3-73cd823aa8b4] The program is delivered through a series of in-person and online sessions conducted over 1-2 years, incorporating readings, presentations, reflections, and discussions.[^chunk:f0216da4-c2cc-5ec1-91b3-73cd823aa8b4]

## Ignatian Principles in Leadership Preparation

All AJCU leadership development programs are grounded in Ignatian spirituality and principles. Programs introduce participants to foundational elements of Ignatian spirituality and pedagogy, and emphasize Ignatian discerning leadership as a core competency.[^chunk:f0216da4-c2cc-5ec1-91b3-73cd823aa8b4] The programs facilitate personal appropriation of Ignatian principles and support leaders in developing their own practice of Ignatian-informed leadership.[^chunk:f0216da4-c2cc-5ec1-91b3-73cd823aa8b4]
