## Overview

The Ignatian Pedagogical Paradigm (IPP) cannot be implemented by distributing a document. Saint Joseph's University's source-of-truth guide is explicit: the paradigm requires educator development because faculty and staff teach through their relationship to knowledge, learners, colleagues, and mission.[^chunk:6b47b6d8-cbae-5d9f-b6f7-2ec82ea750b0] This article draws together everything the guide says about formation opportunities, recognition, resources, co-curricular staff participation, and the sharing of exemplary practice—corresponding to Phase 3 of SJU's implementation roadmap.

---

## Formation Opportunities for Faculty Adopting the IPP

### Guiding Principles

Effective faculty and staff development, according to the SJU guide, should be **voluntary where appropriate, role-sensitive, intellectually credible, and connected to participants' actual work**.[^chunk:6b47b6d8-cbae-5d9f-b6f7-2ec82ea750b0] Formation must not become a loyalty test; mission effectiveness grows through **invitation, intellectual seriousness, protected time, resources, peer leadership, and evidence that the institution aligns workloads and rewards with the educational work it asks people to do**.[^chunk:6b47b6d8-cbae-5d9f-b6f7-2ec82ea750b0]

### The Seven-Stage Formation Sequence

The guide proposes a robust formation sequence with seven stages:[^chunk:6b47b6d8-cbae-5d9f-b6f7-2ec82ea750b0]

| Stage | Content |
|---|---|
| 1. Orientation | History, sources, vocabulary, pluralistic mission, and common misconceptions |
| 2. Experience | Observation of teaching or student-facing work; encounter with students and partners; analysis of one's own practice |
| 3. Design Studio | Redesign of a course, assignment, advising process, team meeting, or policy through the five dynamics |
| 4. Peer Reflection | Disciplined consultation across roles and disciplines |
| 5. Action | A bounded implementation with support |
| 6. Evaluation | Student work, participant reflection, access data, partner feedback, and revision |
| 7. Advanced Practice | Discernment, justice, interculturality, AI, assessment, facilitation, and institutional leadership |

This sequence mirrors the five IPP dynamics applied to the formation process itself: educators encounter the paradigm (experience), examine their practice (reflection), redesign something real (action), and assess results (evaluation) within a clearly understood institutional context.

### The 1993 Source Mandate

The formation imperative is grounded in the paradigm's own source text. *Ignatian Pedagogy: A Practical Approach* (ICAJE, 1993) makes staff development **central**: the paradigm requires practice, shared reflection, and in many cases a change of heart—not merely distribution of a document.[^chunk:6b47b6d8-cbae-5d9f-b6f7-2ec82ea750b0] The guide cites paragraphs 10–11 and 86–92 of the 1993 document as the authoritative basis for this requirement.[^chunk:6b47b6d8-cbae-5d9f-b6f7-2ec82ea750b0]

## Recognition in Promotion and Tenure Decisions

The SJU guide addresses recognition through its Phase 3 roadmap commitment to **align recognition, workload, and resources with the desired work**.[^chunk:514c92fa-878f-5bb4-b7dd-a668e30ffd19] The specific Phase 3 language states that SJU should:

- Incorporate the framework into faculty and staff formation, program review, and selected design processes;
- **Align recognition, workload, and resources with the desired work**; and
- Create exemplars from SJU disciplines and units.[^chunk:514c92fa-878f-5bb4-b7dd-a668e30ffd19]

The guide also warns that the institution teaches through its policies, incentives, technologies, spaces, workloads, partnerships, and decision processes—and that these form people as surely as syllabi do.[^chunk:9a34ed4d-6e02-5417-886f-cc974e519300] An IPP-inspired institutional practice therefore asks what SJU's arrangements cause people to experience, value, and do.[^chunk:9a34ed4d-6e02-5417-886f-cc974e519300]

Equally important is a governance guardrail: SJU **should not use the IPP rubric for personnel judgment without validated purpose, due process, and contextual interpretation**.[^chunk:514c92fa-878f-5bb4-b7dd-a668e30ffd19] This means that while IPP-related course redesign, reflection practice, and community-engaged work should count and be supported, no individual should be evaluated against the rubric in a punitive or compliance-only manner.

In summary, the guide calls for positive alignment of rewards and workload with IPP-related work, while prohibiting the paradigm's use as a surveillance or compliance instrument in personnel decisions.

## Staff in Co-Curricular Roles

### Explicit Extension Beyond the Classroom

The 1993 source document **expressly extends the paradigm beyond the classroom**, and the SJU guide applies it systematically to co-curricular and relational settings.[^chunk:6b47b6d8-cbae-5d9f-b6f7-2ec82ea750b0] Staff in the following roles are specifically named as formative actors:

- **Advising and mentoring**: Advising begins with the student's history, goals, constraints, and evolving identity; creates experiences of exploration; invites reflection on patterns and priorities; supports informed choice; and revisits outcomes.[^chunk:6b47b6d8-cbae-5d9f-b6f7-2ec82ea750b0]
- **Community engagement**: Preparation must include history, partner priorities, power, and role. Reflection must examine both dignity and injustice. Action should be designed *with*, not merely *for*, communities.[^chunk:6b47b6d8-cbae-5d9f-b6f7-2ec82ea750b0]
- **Campus ministry and retreats**: These settings can draw more explicitly from the Spiritual Exercises while keeping participation free.[^chunk:6b47b6d8-cbae-5d9f-b6f7-2ec82ea750b0]
- **Athletics, residence life, and student leadership**: Competition, teamwork, conflict, embodiment, belonging, and leadership create formative experience; structured review can surface motivation, power, failure, resilience, dignity, and responsibility.[^chunk:6b47b6d8-cbae-5d9f-b6f7-2ec82ea750b0]
- **Career and professional development**: Career education can join *utilitas* to *humanitas*, *iustitia*, and *fides* or ultimate meaning.[^chunk:6b47b6d8-cbae-5d9f-b6f7-2ec82ea750b0]

### Participation Expectations

The guide frames co-curricular participation in IPP formation as part of a broader institutional commitment. Phase 2 of the roadmap calls for **voluntary pilots across different schools, modalities, and co-curricular units**.[^chunk:514c92fa-878f-5bb4-b7dd-a668e30ffd19] Phase 3 calls for incorporation of the framework into faculty **and staff** formation.[^chunk:514c92fa-878f-5bb4-b7dd-a668e30ffd19] Participation is presented as invitational and developmental, though institutional expectations remain clear **where mission responsibilities are formally assigned**.[^chunk:514c92fa-878f-5bb4-b7dd-a668e30ffd19]

The seven-stage formation sequence described above is explicitly designed for **faculty and staff** together, with the design studio stage including advising processes and team meetings—not only academic courses.[^chunk:6b47b6d8-cbae-5d9f-b6f7-2ec82ea750b0]

## Resources to Support Course and Process Redesign

### Funded Redesign, Not Just Workshops

The guide is explicit that resource support must go beyond training events. Phase 2 of the implementation roadmap calls for SJU to **fund course and process redesign, not merely workshops**.[^chunk:514c92fa-878f-5bb4-b7dd-a668e30ffd19] Gathering baseline and implementation evidence and including community partners and students in appropriate evaluation are also resourced activities under Phase 2.[^chunk:514c92fa-878f-5bb4-b7dd-a668e30ffd19]

### The IPP Design Canvas

The guide provides a structured **IPP design canvas** that faculty and staff can use for a class, course, program, advising process, co-curricular experience, policy, or institutional initiative.[^chunk:a6c62be7-4932-5dfc-b4ea-52911d9de940] The canvas walks designers through six sections:

- **Purpose and horizon**: What academic, professional, human, and public goods are sought?
- **Context**: Who are the learners and other affected persons? What histories, power relations, technologies, and accessibility requirements apply?
- **Experience**: What will learners encounter? What preparation is necessary?
- **Reflection**: What evidence and concepts will learners use? How will reflection avoid compulsory disclosure?
- **Action**: What decision, creation, practice, communication, service, or commitment follows?
- **Evaluation and next cycle**: What evidence demonstrates mastery and judgment? What should change next?[^chunk:a6c62be7-4932-5dfc-b4ea-52911d9de940]

### The Design-Review Rubric

A **design-review rubric** assesses courses and processes across six dimensions—Purpose, Context, Experience, Reflection, Action, Evaluation, Relationship, and Evidence—at Emerging, Developing, and Strong levels.[^chunk:514c92fa-878f-5bb4-b7dd-a668e30ffd19] The rubric evaluates a **design**, not the virtue of an educator or student, and is explicitly intended to support peer consultation and improvement, not compliance scoring.[^chunk:514c92fa-878f-5bb4-b7dd-a668e30ffd19]

### The Source-of-Truth Document Itself

The guide is structured as a multi-level resource:[^chunk:9a34ed4d-6e02-5417-886f-cc974e519300]

- **Orientation sections** establish common understanding;
- **Design sections** support faculty, staff, and program work;
- **Governance sections** help leaders decide when a proposed use is faithful, overstated, or in need of evidence.

It includes a glossary, annotated primary-source guide, and a common-questions section addressing practical implementation concerns.[^chunk:514c92fa-878f-5bb4-b7dd-a668e30ffd19]

### Institutional Congruence as a Resource Condition

The guide frames resource alignment as one of eight institutional commitments SJU should adopt if it embraces the IPP. Commitment 8 states: **policies, resources, incentives, and technologies support the educational commitments they proclaim**.[^chunk:a6c62be7-4932-5dfc-b4ea-52911d9de940] Without this, formation development cannot be sustained.

## Identifying and Sharing Exemplar Courses and Programs

### Phase 3 Commitment

Phase 3 of the implementation roadmap specifically calls for SJU to **create exemplars from SJU disciplines and units**.[^chunk:514c92fa-878f-5bb4-b7dd-a668e30ffd19] This is paired with connecting IPP evidence to existing assessment and accreditation processes and reviewing the source-of-truth document on a published cycle.[^chunk:514c92fa-878f-5bb4-b7dd-a668e30ffd19]

### Curriculum Mapping as an Identification Tool

At the program level, the guide recommends that programs create a **curriculum map** identifying:[^chunk:6b47b6d8-cbae-5d9f-b6f7-2ec82ea750b0]

- Introduction, reinforcement, and advanced demonstration of key capabilities;
- **Signature experiences and who can access them**;
- Structured reflection before, during, and after experience;
- Increasing authenticity and consequence in action;
- Points where core learning and professional learning are integrated;
- Advising and co-curricular contributions; and
- Program evidence, including alumni, employer, licensure, community-partner, and student data.[^chunk:6b47b6d8-cbae-5d9f-b6f7-2ec82ea750b0]

Signature experiences and their access conditions are thus a primary mechanism for identifying and surfacing exemplary practice.

### Phase 1 Groundwork

Exemplar identification begins in Phase 1, which calls for SJU to **identify where existing SJU practice already embodies the paradigm**.[^chunk:514c92fa-878f-5bb4-b7dd-a668e30ffd19] This prevents the mistaken assumption that IPP work must start from scratch and creates a foundation of recognized existing good practice.

### Evidence-Based Sharing

The guide calls for an SJU evidence agenda combining **student work, course and program assessment, belonging and access data, qualitative accounts, partner feedback, faculty learning, alumni outcomes, and institutional indicators**.[^chunk:514c92fa-878f-5bb4-b7dd-a668e30ffd19] Exemplars are to be grounded in this evidence rather than in anecdote or self-certification. The guide also warns that **labeling a course "IPP" does not itself cause formation**; evaluation should study the design actually implemented, not the label.[^chunk:514c92fa-878f-5bb4-b7dd-a668e30ffd19]

### Peer Reflection as a Sharing Mechanism

The formation sequence itself includes **peer reflection**—disciplined consultation across roles and disciplines—as Stage 4.[^chunk:6b47b6d8-cbae-5d9f-b6f7-2ec82ea750b0] This cross-disciplinary and cross-role structure is a vehicle through which exemplar practices can be documented and circulated.

## Governance Guardrails

The guide sets clear boundaries on formation and recognition practices:

- SJU **should not certify individuals as "Ignatian" based on a short course**.[^chunk:514c92fa-878f-5bb4-b7dd-a668e30ffd19]
- SJU should not **require performative mission language in every syllabus**.[^chunk:514c92fa-878f-5bb4-b7dd-a668e30ffd19]
- The IPP rubric **should not be used for personnel judgment without validated purpose, due process, and contextual interpretation**.[^chunk:514c92fa-878f-5bb4-b7dd-a668e30ffd19]
- Implementation should be **invitational, developmental, and evidence-generating** while institutional expectations remain clear where mission responsibilities are formally assigned.[^chunk:514c92fa-878f-5bb4-b7dd-a668e30ffd19]
