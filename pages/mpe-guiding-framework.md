# Guiding Framework: Characteristics of Jesuit Higher Education

The 2021 *Characteristics of Jesuit Higher Education: A Guide for Mission Reflection* represents an updated framework developed by the Association of Jesuit Colleges and Universities (AJCU) and the Jesuit Conference to guide North American Jesuit institutions in their Mission Priority Examen (MPE) process.[^chunk:6855f939-6c7c-5ec8-9e40-b24804c193a8] This revision follows the first complete cycle of MPE self-studies and peer reviews conducted between 2016 and 2020.[^chunk:6855f939-6c7c-5ec8-9e40-b24804c193a8]

## How the 2021 Update Advances Earlier Frameworks

The 2021 guide builds upon the 2013 document *Some Characteristics of Jesuit Colleges and Universities* by incorporating new institutional learnings and evolved priorities of the Society of Jesus.[^chunk:6855f939-6c7c-5ec8-9e40-b24804c193a8] Specifically, the revision responds to several important developments:

**Integration of New Jesuit Priorities**: The framework now emphasizes the theme of reconciliation from General Congregation 36 and incorporates the Society of Jesus's Universal Apostolic Preferences (UAPs), which provide overarching orientation for all Jesuit works.[^chunk:6855f939-6c7c-5ec8-9e40-b24804c193a8] The UAPs include commitments to show the way to God through Spiritual Exercises and discernment; walk with the poor and marginalized in a mission of reconciliation and justice; accompany young people in creating a hope-filled future; and collaborate in caring for the Common Home.[^chunk:6855f939-6c7c-5ec8-9e40-b24804c193a8]

**Clarification of Mission Focus**: The document reaffirms that "the noun 'university' and the adjective 'Jesuit' always remain fully honored," emphasizing that Jesuit and Catholic identity are defining characteristics rather than ancillary features.[^chunk:6855f939-6c7c-5ec8-9e40-b24804c193a8] Importantly, the framework clarifies that Jesuit identity does not depend on the presence of Jesuits on campus, but rather on demonstrated institutional commitment to living out Jesuit mission and values.[^chunk:6855f939-6c7c-5ec8-9e40-b24804c193a8]

**Emphasis on Anti-Racism and Inclusion**: The 2021 guide significantly strengthens emphasis on building antiracist and inclusive institutions in the North American context.[^chunk:283c0e7f-088b-505f-a3d8-303ada6dcd98]

## Seven Key Characteristics

The framework organizes Jesuit higher education around seven interconnected characteristics:

### Characteristic 1: Leadership and Public Commitment to Mission
Jesuit universities must explicitly communicate their identity as active participants in the Jesuit, Catholic intellectual tradition. This requires intentional development of trustees, presidents, and senior leaders who can articulate and lead the Jesuit mission.[^chunk:283c0e7f-088b-505f-a3d8-303ada6dcd98]

### Characteristic 2: The Academic Life
The university's mission must be embedded in and evident through its academic programs. Grounded in their Ignatian tradition, Jesuit colleges and universities seek to offer an education that transforms students and helps them "be converted to humanity."[^chunk:9a2bfa6d-0e7a-5783-b882-9daf45c865e5] The Ignatian Pedagogical Paradigm (IPP)—featuring components of Context, Experience, Reflection, Action, and Evaluation—guides faculty in fostering critical thinking and responsible citizenship.[^chunk:9a2bfa6d-0e7a-5783-b882-9daf45c865e5]

### Characteristic 3: The Pursuit of Faith, Justice, and Reconciliation
Jesuit universities are called to be a "project of social transformation" amid the pervasive reality of racism, the imperative to care for the planet, and other pressing moral issues.[^chunk:76df12c6-2ab2-5317-ba66-9659fbf3f19f] This characteristic encompasses four interrelated commitments:

**Social Justice and Reconciliation**: The University advances a particular understanding of social justice grounded in Catholic social teaching, including the inherent dignity of all people, environmental justice, a preferential option for those experiencing poverty and marginalization, and the right to life in its fullness.[^chunk:76df12c6-2ab2-5317-ba66-9659fbf3f19f] In this context, justice is understood "not simply as retribution or restitution" but as "a right relationship with God, the earth, and other creatures," with reconciliation as the guiding star.[^chunk:76df12c6-2ab2-5317-ba66-9659fbf3f19f]

**Racial Justice, Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion**: Jesuit universities must speak truthfully about the institution's and Church's history with respect to racism, including colonialism, white supremacy, and exclusion.[^chunk:76df12c6-2ab2-5317-ba66-9659fbf3f19f] A comprehensive plan to address racial injustice should include dialogue about cultural differences and de-centering white privilege; commitment to racial equity and inclusion by leadership; diversifying the campus community; developing skills for ongoing conversations about anti-racism; and, most importantly, recruiting and retaining members of underrepresented communities in the student body, faculty, administration, and staff.[^chunk:76df12c6-2ab2-5317-ba66-9659fbf3f19f]

**Care for Our Common Home**: The Jesuit and Catholic University commits itself to work for environmental justice and sustainability, promoting awareness of the dangers of over-consumption, resource misuse, and pollution.[^chunk:76df12c6-2ab2-5317-ba66-9659fbf3f19f] All groups within the University should aspire to address environmental degradation, with the curriculum, research, and advocacy reflecting this commitment.[^chunk:bf606938-d1a0-537f-82e1-483d0a08d9bb]

**Service to the Global Community**: Universities provide opportunities to discuss how the moral grounding for social justice concerns intersects with institutional choices, and seek ways their operations, policies, and programs advance deeper understanding of social justice and reconciliation.[^chunk:76df12c6-2ab2-5317-ba66-9659fbf3f19f]

### Characteristic 4: Promoting an Ignatian Campus Culture
Jesuit universities rely on the Ignatian spiritual tradition to develop a campus culture characterized by respect for all persons, thoughtful dialogue, reconciliation, and compassion.[^chunk:bf606938-d1a0-537f-82e1-483d0a08d9bb] This includes vibrant liturgical life through Campus Ministry, retreats, spiritual direction, and programs grounded in Ignatian discernment.[^chunk:bf606938-d1a0-537f-82e1-483d0a08d9bb]

### Characteristic 5: Service to the Church
Jesuit universities contribute unique resources to build up the local and universal Church through education and formation of students, research and teaching, and advancement of the Catholic intellectual and social traditions.[^chunk:80ad288c-d3b5-5860-bd2e-020343ce5998]

### Characteristic 6: Relationship to the Society of Jesus
The University lives out its Jesuit identity through collaboration with individual Jesuits, campus Jesuit Communities, the Province, and national and international Jesuit networks.[^chunk:80ad288c-d3b5-5860-bd2e-020343ce5998]

### Characteristic 7: Institutional Integrity
Jesuit and Catholic Universities must manage themselves with the highest standards of ethics and care for all community members, addressing policies and practices related to just compensation, equity, inclusion, elimination of bias, and care for the common home.[^chunk:13e22662-b4b7-5bea-bedd-053e64046ff0]

## The Mission Priority Examen Process

The MPE is a collaborative self-study and peer review process conducted every seven years to reaffirm the Jesuit and Catholic mission of North American institutions.[^chunk:e645f4fd-a362-5cce-bdde-fdab0c0614be] The process follows eight steps, beginning with agreement by the Provincial and institution on focus and scope, through assignment of committees, completion and submission of self-study, peer team visit and report, final submission of materials, consideration by the Society of Jesus, campus communication, and ongoing follow-up.[^chunk:e645f4fd-a362-5cce-bdde-fdab0c0614be]

The university's portion should be completed within two semesters, with the entire process including Jesuit consideration extending through the following year.[^chunk:e645f4fd-a362-5cce-bdde-fdab0c0614be] Upon completion, Fr. General writes a letter to the Provincial outlining his decision regarding the school's Catholic and Jesuit identity and any areas requiring attention.[^chunk:e645f4fd-a362-5cce-bdde-fdab0c0614be]

## Commitment to Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion

The 2021 framework makes explicit commitments to diversity, equity, and inclusion as central to Jesuit mission rather than peripheral concerns. Jesuit universities are "always seeking to diversify their student populations and create equitable and inclusive academic environments," requiring meaningful changes in who teaches, what is taught, and how teaching is conducted.[^chunk:9a2bfa6d-0e7a-5783-b882-9daf45c865e5]

Institutional discussions on mission are expected to be clearly tied to commitments to diversity, equity, and inclusion, with the University's Jesuit mission and Catholic identity "recognizably and thoughtfully presented" in bylaws, promotional materials, and campus physicality that reflect commitment to a diverse, inclusive, and equitable world.[^chunk:9a2bfa6d-0e7a-5783-b882-9daf45c865e5]

When recruiting faculty and staff, universities should represent to all potential applicants that they are both Jesuit and Catholic, offering candidates opportunities and resources for exploring the school's mission during the hiring process.[^chunk:9a2bfa6d-0e7a-5783-b882-9daf45c865e5]

## Social Justice and Environmental Stewardship

Social justice and environmental stewardship are not ancillary to the Jesuit university mission but rather central to it. The framework emphasizes that these commitments "follow logically from the UAPs and from Catholic social thought, which orients all Jesuit works toward the creation of a more just and loving world," and "should be embraced by all groups and communities within the University."[^chunk:76df12c6-2ab2-5317-ba66-9659fbf3f19f]

The Society of Jesus's foundational principle states: "The service of faith and the promotion of justice cannot be for us simply one ministry among others. It must be the integrating factor of all our ministries."[^chunk:76df12c6-2ab2-5317-ba66-9659fbf3f19f]
