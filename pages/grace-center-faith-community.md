## Overview

The Grace Center for Faith and Community is a newly established center at Le Moyne College that was created during the 2025 spring semester.[^chunk:6ec9ec6f-f12f-5598-badc-1ecd56035dab] Located in the Panasci Family Chapel, the center encompasses mission integration, campus ministry, cultural and social engagement, faith formation and the Le Moyne Way.[^chunk:6ec9ec6f-f12f-5598-badc-1ecd56035dab]

## Mission and Vision

The Grace Center represents Le Moyne's commitment to its Jesuit mission and social justice with a renewed organizational structure and personnel.[^chunk:6ec9ec6f-f12f-5598-badc-1ecd56035dab] According to President Linda LeMura, "The work of the Grace Center is a reflection of Le Moyneʼs unwavering commitment to educate and care for the whole person – mind, body and spirit – while cultivating a campus community rooted in reflection, purpose and a shared responsibility for one another."[^chunk:6ec9ec6f-f12f-5598-badc-1ecd56035dab] The center aims to raise the involvement of students, faculty, administrators, alumni and community partners to strengthen Le Moyne's identity as a Jesuit institution committed to service, faith and forming leaders who are compassionate, inclusive and grounded in social justice.[^chunk:6ec9ec6f-f12f-5598-badc-1ecd56035dab]

## Founders

The Grace Center was created following a gift from Katherine "Kathy" Grace Purcell ʼ66 and her late husband John Purcell ʼ65.[^chunk:6ec9ec6f-f12f-5598-badc-1ecd56035dab] Kathy Purcell reflected on the center's establishment: "Knowing that the Grace Center will contribute so significantly to spirituality, faith formation and Le Moyneʼs Jesuit mission brings me great joy! Those were each a vital part of the experience that John and I valued during our time at Le Moyne."[^chunk:6ec9ec6f-f12f-5598-badc-1ecd56035dab]
