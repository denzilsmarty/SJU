## Overview

Mission assessment and regional accreditation are interconnected processes through which institutions like Saint Joseph's University demonstrate institutional effectiveness and compliance with established standards. The integration of these processes requires systematic documentation, evidence gathering, and stakeholder engagement.

## Regional Accreditation and the Self-Study Process

The Middle States Commission on Higher Education (MSCHE) establishes the framework for regional accreditation through a structured self-study process[^chunk:9ef8b3a2-2525-5569-ab21-49205413507a]. Saint Joseph's University undergoes re-accreditation on an eight-year cycle, with the accreditation process culminating in a formal determination by the Commission[^chunk:9ef8b3a2-2525-5569-ab21-49205413507a].

## Role of Institutional Self-Study

The institutional self-study serves as the primary mechanism for demonstrating compliance with accreditation standards and requirements. The self-study is a multi-year re-accreditation process that produces a comprehensive Self-Study Report[^chunk:9ef8b3a2-2525-5569-ab21-49205413507a]. This report must demonstrate institutional compliance with the Standards for Accreditation and Requirements for Affiliation with Middle States[^chunk:9ef8b3a2-2525-5569-ab21-49205413507a].

The self-study process is guided by institutional self-study priorities and outcomes determined from community input[^chunk:9ef8b3a2-2525-5569-ab21-49205413507a]. These priorities frame the narrative of the Self-Study Report through which compliance to the accreditation standards is exhibited[^chunk:9ef8b3a2-2525-5569-ab21-49205413507a].

## Organizational Structure and Governance

Institutional integration of accreditation requirements is facilitated through a formal governance structure. The self-study process is led by the Provost and co-chairs, supported by a steering committee and working groups[^chunk:9ef8b3a2-2525-5569-ab21-49205413507a]. This structure ensures diverse institutional participation, including students, faculty, staff, administrators, alumni, and the Board of Trustees[^chunk:9ef8b3a2-2525-5569-ab21-49205413507a].

Working groups are organized around specific accreditation standards, with each group composed of faculty, staff, and students[^chunk:9ef8b3a2-2525-5569-ab21-49205413507a]. Standard areas include:

- Design & Delivery of the Student Learning Experience[^chunk:9ef8b3a2-2525-5569-ab21-49205413507a]

- Education Effectiveness Assessment[^chunk:9ef8b3a2-2525-5569-ab21-49205413507a]

## Documentation and Evidence Gathering

Institutional assessment infrastructure supports evidence collection for accreditation purposes. The Office of Institutional Effectiveness plays a central role in coordinating the accreditation process, with specific roles assigned to support evidence inventory and verification of compliance[^chunk:9ef8b3a2-2525-5569-ab21-49205413507a].

Key personnel responsible for documentation include an Accreditation and Academic Compliance Officer who oversees evidence inventory, and a Chief Compliance and Risk Management Officer who manages verification of compliance[^chunk:9ef8b3a2-2525-5569-ab21-49205413507a].

## Timeline and Accreditation Determination

Institutions follow a structured timeline for the self-study process. Saint Joseph's University completed its self-study process with a final Self-Study Report prepared for accreditation review[^chunk:9ef8b3a2-2525-5569-ab21-49205413507a]. Following submission of the self-study, the Commission makes a formal accreditation determination[^chunk:9ef8b3a2-2525-5569-ab21-49205413507a].

Throughout the self-study process, the steering committee provides periodic progress summaries to maintain transparency and keep the university community informed of progress[^chunk:9ef8b3a2-2525-5569-ab21-49205413507a].

## Integration of Mission and Accreditation Assessment

The integration of mission assessment with accreditation requirements occurs through the self-study priorities framework. By grounding the self-study in institutional priorities derived from community input, universities align their mission-centered assessment activities with accreditation compliance demonstrations. This integration ensures that institutional effectiveness assessment serves both internal mission-focused improvement and external accreditation accountability purposes.
