## Overview

Between 1973 and 1980, Superior General Pedro Arrupe, S.J., delivered two addresses that fundamentally reoriented how the Society of Jesus understood the purpose of education. Taken together with General Congregation 32's landmark Decree 4 (1975), these interventions insisted that faith and justice were inseparable, that technical excellence without humanity was a danger, and that Jesuit education must form people who serve others rather than advance themselves. This turn toward justice remains one of the most cited reference points in contemporary Jesuit educational discourse.
