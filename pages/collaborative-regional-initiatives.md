## Overview

The Association of Jesuit Colleges and Universities (AJCU) serves as a coordinating body for collaborative initiatives among its member institutions. This network facilitates mission-focused work and institutional advancement across Jesuit higher education.

## AJCU Member Network

The Association of Jesuit Colleges and Universities comprises a substantial network of institutions committed to Jesuit Catholic education. Member institutions include Boston College, Fairfield University, John Carroll University, Loyola University Maryland, Rockhurst University, Santa Clara University, The University of Scranton, Canisius University, Fordham University, Le Moyne College, Loyola University New Orleans, Saint Joseph's University, Seattle University, University of Detroit Mercy, College of the Holy Cross, Georgetown University, Loyola Marymount University, Marquette University, Saint Louis University, Spring Hill College, University of San Francisco, Creighton University, Gonzaga University, Loyola University Chicago, Regis University, Saint Peter's University, St. John's College (Belize), and Xavier University.[^chunk:eaf1eace-402b-509d-869f-6ac8e6891925] The association also includes associate members such as Campion College, Newman College, Regis College, and St. Mark's College.[^chunk:eaf1eace-402b-509d-869f-6ac8e6891925]

## Coordinated Initiatives and Programs

AJCU facilitates coordinated initiatives among member institutions through several established programs and networks. These include FACHEX, Jesuit Excellence Tours (JET), Networks, and a Job Bank that support collaboration and knowledge-sharing across member campuses.[^chunk:eaf1eace-402b-509d-869f-6ac8e6891925]

## Mission-Focused Collaborative Work

Jesuit colleges demonstrate commitment to mission-focused collaboration through structured institutional processes. A key collaborative initiative is the Mission Priority Examen, a process required of all Jesuit colleges and universities to explore areas of strength and weakness in institutional mission.[^chunk:28ee9d8b-f65d-5276-83ba-a2e0ad82024b] This peer-reviewed process involves faculty, staff, and students providing input on mission priorities, with peer reviewers from other institutions evaluating findings.[^chunk:28ee9d8b-f65d-5276-83ba-a2e0ad82024b]

Institutions completing the Examen identify institutional priorities addressing Jesuit identity and mission. Common focus areas include comprehensive Jesuit Identity Initiatives, mission-based commitments to diversity and equity, hiring for mission formation, education about Ignatian spirituality and Jesuit pedagogy, and integration of mission identity across curricula.[^chunk:28ee9d8b-f65d-5276-83ba-a2e0ad82024b]

## Response to Institutional Challenges

Jesuit colleges collaborate in addressing significant institutional challenges affecting the broader Church and higher education. Some institutions have appointed commissions addressing response to the Catholic sexual abuse crisis, with goals including standing in solidarity with victim-survivors, healing harm inflicted by abuse, and addressing systemic institutional and social failures.[^chunk:28ee9d8b-f65d-5276-83ba-a2e0ad82024b]
