## Overview

General Congregation 34 (GC 34) was a landmark assembly of the Society of Jesus held in 1995. Its **Decree 17**, titled *"Jesuits and University Life"*, remains the authoritative congregational statement on the purpose, responsibilities, and self-critical obligations of Jesuit universities. It is a foundational Tier 1 source governing how the Jesuit higher-education tradition understands teaching, research, formation, and institutional accountability.[^chunk:9a34ed4d-6e02-5417-886f-cc974e519300]

---

## Purpose of Jesuit Universities According to GC 34 Decree 17

Decree 17 affirmed the Jesuit university as a distinctive institutional setting called to pursue several interlocking goods simultaneously. According to the decree, a Jesuit university is a site for:

- **rigorous teaching** across disciplines;
- **research** conducted with intellectual seriousness;
- **interdisciplinary work** that crosses the boundaries of specialization;
- **service** to wider society;
- **pastoral care** of persons within the institution; and
- **the integration of faith and justice** as a unified mission commitment rather than parallel activities.[^chunk:9a34ed4d-6e02-5417-886f-cc974e519300]

This framing reflects the broader Society of Jesus mission as defined by General Congregation 32's Decree 4 (1975), which established that the service of faith and the promotion of justice are inseparable.[^chunk:9a34ed4d-6e02-5417-886f-cc974e519300] GC 34 carried that commitment into the specific institutional context of the university.

The decree is listed among the minimum primary-source corpus required for any serious engagement with Jesuit higher education and Ignatian pedagogy, alongside the *Spiritual Exercises*, the *Constitutions*, and the major addresses of Superior Generals Arrupe, Kolvenbach, Nicolás, and Sosa.[^chunk:9a34ed4d-6e02-5417-886f-cc974e519300]

## Integration of Teaching, Research, and Formation

GC 34 Decree 17 did not treat teaching, research, and human formation as separate departmental concerns. Rather, it located them within a single institutional vocation. The decree's vision was developed and extended by subsequent Superior General addresses:

- **Peter-Hans Kolvenbach** (Santa Clara address, 2000) shifted the evaluative standard for a Jesuit university from *what students know* to *who they become*, arguing that educated solidarity normally requires contact with the suffering of others joined to intellectual inquiry and moral reflection.[^chunk:9a34ed4d-6e02-5417-886f-cc974e519300]
- Kolvenbach (2001) also recovered a fourfold account of university purpose associated with Diego de Ledesma: *utilitas* (practical and professional usefulness), *iustitia* (justice and the common good), *humanitas* (full human development), and *fides* (faith and ultimate meaning). These purposes give higher education a horizon within which teaching and research are oriented toward formation.[^chunk:9a34ed4d-6e02-5417-886f-cc974e519300]
- **Adolfo Nicolás** (2010) named the "globalization of superficiality" as a threat to the depth of thought and imagination that genuine integration requires.[^chunk:9a34ed4d-6e02-5417-886f-cc974e519300]
- **Arturo Sosa** (2018) located the Jesuit university within a mission of reconciliation—with God, with one another, and with creation—and identified the digital ecosystem as a new cultural paradigm requiring institutional response.[^chunk:6b47b6d8-cbae-5d9f-b6f7-2ec82ea750b0]

The Ignatian Pedagogical Paradigm (IPP), promulgated in 1993, is the principal practical instrument through which this integrated vision reaches individual courses and programs. GC 34's higher-education decree provides the congregational authority within which the IPP operates at the university level.[^chunk:9a34ed4d-6e02-5417-886f-cc974e519300]

## Obligations Regarding Institutional Self-Evaluation

One of the most distinctive and demanding elements of Decree 17 is its call for **institutional self-examination and accountability**. GC 34 did not permit Jesuit universities to rest on their historical identity or their mission statements. Instead, it called institutions to evaluate whether their *actual priorities*—budgetary, curricular, personnel, and programmatic—genuinely serve the mission described above.[^chunk:9a34ed4d-6e02-5417-886f-cc974e519300]

This obligation has several practical implications that later Jesuit higher-education documents have elaborated:

1. **Self-evaluation is structural, not merely rhetorical.** The question is not whether an institution uses Jesuit language, but whether its arrangements cause people to experience, value, and act in ways consistent with the mission.[^chunk:9a34ed4d-6e02-5417-886f-cc974e519300]
2. **The institution itself teaches.** Policies, incentives, spaces, workloads, partnerships, and decision processes form persons as surely as syllabi do.[^chunk:9a34ed4d-6e02-5417-886f-cc974e519300]
3. **Evidence, not intention, is the test.** Institutional claims about mission require evidence proportional to their strength; good intention does not become self-certifying.[^chunk:514c92fa-878f-5bb4-b7dd-a668e30ffd19]

The IPP's fifth dynamic—**evaluation**—operates at the institutional as well as course level, asking what was learned, what changed, what resulted, and what should happen next, with the explicit expectation that the cycle restarts with the findings.[^chunk:9a34ed4d-6e02-5417-886f-cc974e519300]

## GC 34 in the Hierarchy of Jesuit Sources

Decrees of General Congregations occupy **Tier 1** in the authority hierarchy governing Jesuit educational documents—the foundational and constitutional level alongside the *Spiritual Exercises* and the *Constitutions*. They establish deep grammar, mission, and authoritative commitments to which all institutional and pedagogical documents are subordinate.[^chunk:9a34ed4d-6e02-5417-886f-cc974e519300]

Decree 17 is therefore not merely a historical reference; it remains the controlling congregational text for Jesuit higher education. Official promulgated documents of the Society—including GC 34—take precedence over institutional guides, secondary scholarship, and consultancy models.[^chunk:9a34ed4d-6e02-5417-886f-cc974e519300]
