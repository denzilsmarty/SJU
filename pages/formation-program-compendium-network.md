## Overview

The AJCU Formation Program Compendium and international Jesuit networks represent a coordinated effort to strengthen formation and mission integration across Jesuit higher education institutions. These resources and collaborative structures address the diverse needs of faculty, staff, trustees, and other institutional leaders seeking to deepen their engagement with Jesuit identity and Ignatian spirituality.

## Formation Program Compendium Resources

The AJCU Formation Program Compendium is a searchable directory designed to serve Jesuit colleges and universities in North America.[^chunk:b6d63861-5668-56e7-a6ac-f5b2c81873d0] The Compendium was made possible by a generous grant from the Council for Independent Colleges/Executives in Church-Related Higher Education.[^chunk:b6d63861-5668-56e7-a6ac-f5b2c81873d0]

The resource provides access to over 121 formation programs[^chunk:b6d63861-5668-56e7-a6ac-f5b2c81873d0] with searchable features that allow users to filter by program type, target audience, program length, and institution. Target audiences include faculty, staff/administrators, trustees, and other categories. Program offerings range from spiritual retreats and Ignatian pilgrimages to mission seminars, leadership development initiatives, and spiritual direction training.

AJCU welcomes contributions to the Compendium and aspires to expand it into an international resource reflecting best practices from Jesuit colleges and universities around the world.[^chunk:b6d63861-5668-56e7-a6ac-f5b2c81873d0]

## International Jesuit Networks and Collaborative Formation

### IAJU Task Force for Formation and Mission Integration

Aligned with the IAJU Strategic Agenda 2024–2028, the Task Force for Formation and Mission Integration seeks to strengthen the integration of Jesuit values and Ignatian spirituality into the life of Jesuit universities through reflection and dialogue, formation, and the exchange of experiences and resources.[^chunk:18dfa3b4-7604-55be-873b-9d45ba79b36d]

The Task Force leadership reflects international coordination:[^chunk:18dfa3b4-7604-55be-873b-9d45ba79b36d]
- Fr. Pep Mària, SJ serves as IAJU Co-Chair
- Susana Di Trolio co-chairs the Kircher Network
- Joseph DeFeo leads AJCU-NA (Association of Jesuit Colleges and Universities - North America)
- Fr. Joe Arun, SJ leads JHEASA (Jesuit Higher Education Association of South Asia)
- Lucas Blangino leads AUSJAL (Association of Universities entrusted to the Society of Jesus in Latin America)
- Dr. Benjamin Tolosa, Jr. leads AJCU-AP (AJCU - Asia-Pacific)

### Mission and Objectives

The Task Force pursues three primary objectives:[^chunk:18dfa3b4-7604-55be-873b-9d45ba79b36d]

1. Strengthen formation of faculty, staff, and future leaders in Jesuit identity and mission
2. Leverage and share resources to promote cross-regional synergies and mission-centered development
3. Pilot and scale a global Community of Practice for mission integration across institutions

Faculty and administrative leaders join a global Community of Practice tasked with mission formation that is responsive to diverse socio-political, religious, and cultural contexts.[^chunk:18dfa3b4-7604-55be-873b-9d45ba79b36d]

### Programs and Initiatives

The Task Force coordinates several major programs:[^chunk:18dfa3b4-7604-55be-873b-9d45ba79b36d]

**IAJU Formation and Mission Integration Conference 2027**: The new IAJU Task Force will hold a conference on fostering and witnessing to Jesuit identity and values in universities, scheduled for June 27–30 at Ateneo de Manila University.

**IAJU Best Practices in Jesuit Higher Education**: This common project promotes the sharing of best practices and know-how, fosters academic collaboration, and strengthens connections among colleagues. It includes an Online Letter showcasing curated practices, an Online Magazine, and webinars featuring international experts.

**IAJU Religious Diversity and Secularization (JHERDS)**: A project focused on promoting Jesuit spirituality, pedagogy, and values in Jesuit universities worldwide.

**Kircher Network - Higher Education for Social Transformation (HEST)**: The HEST program brings together Jesuit higher education institutions in Europe and Lebanon to develop research and formation contributing to needed social changes, while building collaborative partnerships with Jesuit Social Centres.[^chunk:18dfa3b4-7604-55be-873b-9d45ba79b36d]

## Role of Regional Associations in Formation Support

Regional associations play a critical coordinating role in supporting institutional formation efforts. The structure established by the IAJU Task Force demonstrates how regional bodies facilitate formation activities:

- **AJCU-NA** coordinates formation efforts across North American Jesuit institutions, including the Formation Program Compendium
- **JHEASA** serves South Asian institutions
- **AUSJAL** coordinates Latin American universities entrusted to the Society of Jesus
- **AJCU-AP** supports Asia-Pacific institutions
- **Kircher Network** facilitates European and Middle Eastern collaboration

These regional associations enable institutions to access shared resources, participate in cross-regional synergies, and contribute to a global Community of Practice while remaining responsive to their particular geographical, cultural, and socio-political contexts.
