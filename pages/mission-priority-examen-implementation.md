# Mission Priority Examen and Institutional Alignment

The Mission Priority Examen (MPE) is a systematic process of institutional discernment designed to help Jesuit colleges and universities in North America assess and strengthen their commitment to their Catholic Jesuit identity. [^chunk:5490550d-e012-5c77-8b39-14c3b2f46b8f]

## Overview and Purpose

During the period between 2021-2026, all Jesuit colleges and universities in North America are invited to engage in the Mission Priority Examen, a process involving Ignatian reflection to identify the strengths and opportunities of the university in regards to its Jesuit, Catholic identity. [^chunk:5490550d-e012-5c77-8b39-14c3b2f46b8f] These strengths and opportunities are subsequently outlined in a campus self-study report and reviewed during a peer team visit. [^chunk:5490550d-e012-5c77-8b39-14c3b2f46b8f]

## How MPE Supports Mission Alignment

The Mission Priority Examen supports institutional mission alignment by creating a structured framework for universities to intentionally integrate, promulgate, and embody their identity as Jesuit Catholic institutions. The process enables institutions to deepen their embrace and expression of their shared mission, positioning themselves to serve as beacons of light and sources of hope in the world. [^chunk:5490550d-e012-5c77-8b39-14c3b2f46b8f]

## Structure and Methodology

The MPE process involves multiple stakeholders and stages:

**Self-Study Development**: The self-study report flows from numerous interviews, group visioning sessions, and planning sessions involving students, faculty, staff, and trustees. [^chunk:5490550d-e012-5c77-8b39-14c3b2f46b8f]

**Peer Team Review**: Following the completion of the self-study, a peer team conducts a visit to evaluate the institution's mission alignment. The peer team is composed of mission and ministry leaders from other Jesuit institutions. [^chunk:5490550d-e012-5c77-8b39-14c3b2f46b8f]

## Strategic Implementation: Xavier University Case Study

Xavier University engaged in the Mission Priority Examen during the 2023-2024 academic year, with a peer team visit held on November 28-30, 2023. [^chunk:5490550d-e012-5c77-8b39-14c3b2f46b8f] The peer team was chaired by Cindy Schmersal, EdD (VP Mission and Ministry, Creighton University), and included Brigid Alba (Assistant Director of Mission Engagement, Marquette University), Bill Krieg, MA (Director of Campus Ministry, Rockhurst University), and Myles Sheehan, SJ, MD (Academic physician, Georgetown University). [^chunk:5490550d-e012-5c77-8b39-14c3b2f46b8f]

### Strategic Decision-Making

Xavier's MPE process coincided with the University's strategic planning process, demonstrating how MPE findings directly inform institutional strategy. [^chunk:5490550d-e012-5c77-8b39-14c3b2f46b8f] Xavier's five identified MPE Priorities flow from the strategic plan, "Xavier 200: The Greater, The Better, The More 2023-2031": [^chunk:5490550d-e012-5c77-8b39-14c3b2f46b8f]

1. **Jesuit College of Osteopathic Medicine**: The establishment, support, and execution of the first Jesuit College of Osteopathic Medicine will align with and boost the Jesuit Catholic mission of the University. [^chunk:5490550d-e012-5c77-8b39-14c3b2f46b8f]

2. **Mission and Ministry Division**: The re-constituted Division of Mission and Ministry will achieve its purpose to enhance the animation of the University's Jesuit Catholic educational identity and activation of the four Universal Apostolic Preferences. [^chunk:5490550d-e012-5c77-8b39-14c3b2f46b8f]

3. **Laudato Si' Commitment**: Feasible goals of the campus' Laudato Si' Action Plan will be identified and advanced to fulfill Xavier's commitment to being a Laudato Si' university. [^chunk:5490550d-e012-5c77-8b39-14c3b2f46b8f]

4. **Ignatian Vocational Discernment**: Develop and implement a model of Ignatian vocational discernment for second year students. [^chunk:5490550d-e012-5c77-8b39-14c3b2f46b8f]

5. **Institutional Health Performance**: Actions and initiatives to advance on key performance indices of institutional health for Leadership in Mission and Identity will be undertaken, including student mission learning, faculty/staff mission professional development distinction, and www.JesuitResource.org engagement. [^chunk:5490550d-e012-5c77-8b39-14c3b2f46b8f]
