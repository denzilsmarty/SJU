## Overview

The Mission Priority Examen (MPE) operates through a collaborative governance framework involving Jesuit leadership, AJCU institutions, university administrations, and peer communities. This structure ensures that the process remains faithful to Ignatian principles while maintaining accountability to the Society of Jesus and supporting institutional mission development.[^chunk:1b8e170f-7c0a-503a-9b71-3cba6453303e]

## Key Governance Bodies

### The Society of Jesus and Provincial Leadership

The MPE is established at the request of the Jesuit Superior General by the North American Provincials and the AJCU presidents.[^chunk:1b8e170f-7c0a-503a-9b71-3cba6453303e] The Superior General, currently V. Rev. Arturo Sosa, S.J., serves as the international leader of the Society and is the recipient of each school's MPE materials. His review of submitted materials leads to his decision to reaffirm the school's Jesuit, Catholic identity and to offer specific guidance to institutional leaders.[^chunk:1e96400d-3644-5cc2-bc81-6536c66e0697]

The Jesuit Conference of Canada and the United States (JCCU) coordinates and supports the work of the Society of Jesus in North America through a staffed office in Washington, D.C.[^chunk:b82d9f12-b446-5720-aa05-dc81470da9b4] Within the JCCU are five Provinces, each headed by a regional Provincial.[^chunk:b82d9f12-b446-5720-aa05-dc81470da9b4] A year or more before the MPE process launches, a Jesuit Provincial invites a school to participate, allowing the institution to tailor the experience to its own needs and circumstances.[^chunk:b82d9f12-b446-5720-aa05-dc81470da9b4]

### Provincial Assistants for Higher Education (PAHE)

The Provincial's staff includes a Provincial Assistant for Higher Education (PAHE), who functions as the Provincial's liaison and representative in the MPE process, though the Provincial maintains oversight in all matters.[^chunk:b82d9f12-b446-5720-aa05-dc81470da9b4] The PAHE works in consultation with the President to align on the process and coordinates with AJCU to invite the Peer Visitor Team.[^chunk:d7983857-9c88-5df3-9729-b449b3d8acfe] The PAHE follows implementation of the mission priorities through annual visits and stands ready to provide tailored resources.[^chunk:1e96400d-3644-5cc2-bc81-6536c66e0697]

### Association of Jesuit Colleges and Universities (AJCU)

The AJCU is composed of 28 U.S. and Belizean Jesuit institutions of higher education and their Canadian partners.[^chunk:b82d9f12-b446-5720-aa05-dc81470da9b4] The Association's interim executive director represents AJCU schools publicly, while the AJCU Vice President for Mission Integration coordinates the MPE process overall.[^chunk:b82d9f12-b446-5720-aa05-dc81470da9b4] AJCU facilitates the process and works jointly with the Province to appoint the Peer Visitor Team.[^chunk:d7983857-9c88-5df3-9729-b449b3d8acfe]

## University-Level Governance

### Presidential Leadership

The President is the internal leader and public face of the college or university.[^chunk:b82d9f12-b446-5720-aa05-dc81470da9b4] In the context of the MPE, the President appoints the Self-study Committee and its chairs, works with the PAHE to select the Peer Visitor Team, meets with the local Bishop, and leads the school's efforts to implement the Jesuit Mission Priorities that have been established.[^chunk:b82d9f12-b446-5720-aa05-dc81470da9b4] The President shares the Superior General's decision with trustees, the campus community, and stakeholders.[^chunk:1e96400d-3644-5cc2-bc81-6536c66e0697]

### The Self-Study Committee

The Self-study Committee is appointed by the school's President and is tasked with reviewing recent Jesuit, Catholic Mission Priorities, engaging campus community members in conversations about the school's future mission commitments (especially with respect to areas emphasized by the Provincial), recommending specific Jesuit Mission Priorities, and hosting the Peer Visitor Team.[^chunk:b82d9f12-b446-5720-aa05-dc81470da9b4] The Committee conducts interviews, focus groups, surveys, and other activities to inform institutional reflection and priority-setting.[^chunk:b82d9f12-b446-5720-aa05-dc81470da9b4]

The Self-study Committee is responsible for writing a Self-study Report in the spirit of an Ignatian Examen, expressing institutional successes and challenges in a spirit of freedom and hope.[^chunk:b82d9f12-b446-5720-aa05-dc81470da9b4] The Committee reflects gratefully on the school's current Jesuit, Catholic mission commitments and the experiences of colleagues and students, designs an approach to the Examen process consistent with the school's circumstances and culture, and considers mission reflections from students, faculty, staff, members of the Jesuit Community, trustees, alumni/ae, and the local Bishop.[^chunk:d7983857-9c88-5df3-9729-b449b3d8acfe]

### Board of Trustees

Trustees are apprised at the start of the MPE process, updated regularly, and may meet the peer team.[^chunk:1e96400d-3644-5cc2-bc81-6536c66e0697] The President names the Self-study Committee with input from faculty, staff, students, alumni, and trustees.[^chunk:b82d9f12-b446-5720-aa05-dc81470da9b4]

## The Peer Visiting Process

### Peer Visitor Teams

Peer Visitor Teams (also referred to as Peer Reviewers) are knowledgeable colleagues from other Jesuit colleges and universities who have volunteered their time to review the school's MPE materials, conduct on-campus discussions, and write a Peer Report about the school's Jesuit Mission Priorities.[^chunk:b82d9f12-b446-5720-aa05-dc81470da9b4] The Peer Team is jointly appointed by AJCU and the Province.[^chunk:d7983857-9c88-5df3-9729-b449b3d8acfe] These teams consist of three to four colleagues from other AJCU schools who spend two to four days on campus as companions in mission, meeting the President, leaders, faculty, staff, students, alumni, and trustees.[^chunk:1b8e170f-7c0a-503a-9b71-3cba6453303e]

The Peer Team reads the self-study and engages in structured listening, serving as companions in mission who wish to see the school's Jesuit, Catholic mission thrive and grow in every dimension of its work.[^chunk:d7983857-9c88-5df3-9729-b449b3d8acfe] A draft report follows the campus visit, and an iterative exchange may revise either the report or the self-study, with disagreements noted in writing.[^chunk:1b8e170f-7c0a-503a-9b71-3cba6453303e]

## External Stakeholder Engagement

### The Local Bishop

Every school is expected to solicit the local Bishop's input, with the President determining how and when.[^chunk:1e96400d-3644-5cc2-bc81-6536c66e0697] Input from the local Bishop regarding how the school can better serve the local Church is a collaborative element of the MPE.[^chunk:d7983857-9c88-5df3-9729-b449b3d8acfe]

## Collaborative Structure and Mission Integration

The MPE is intentionally framed as a collaborative, Ignatian experience occurring at multiple levels: among students, faculty, staff, and trustees in the college or university community; with other Jesuit institutions who generously make colleagues available to serve as Peer Visitors; with the Jesuit Province that sponsors the MPE; and with the Association of Jesuit Colleges and Universities, which facilitates the process.[^chunk:d7983857-9c88-5df3-9729-b449b3d8acfe]

The process operates under the principle that it is both evaluative and developmental—an honest look at institutional life ordered to growth, not an audit ordered to compliance.[^chunk:1b8e170f-7c0a-503a-9b71-3cba6453303e] The Society of Jesus and AJCU affirm a process that is institutionally reflective, sensitive to the distinctive circumstances and potential of each school, and deeply connected to the mission.[^chunk:d7983857-9c88-5df3-9729-b449b3d8acfe]
