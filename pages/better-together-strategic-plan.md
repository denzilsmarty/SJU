# Carroll College Strategic Plan: Better Together Initiative

## Overview

Better Together is the strategic plan of the Carroll College of Arts and Sciences at Le Moyne College, adopted on February 1, 2026.[^chunk:55fe5aca-57eb-5445-9af3-4e0625ce10aa] The plan advances the larger institution-wide vision of Tomorrow Together, which is inspired by Le Moyne College's identity as a Jesuit, Catholic liberal arts college.[^chunk:55fe5aca-57eb-5445-9af3-4e0625ce10aa]

## Mission, Vision, and Values

### Mission

The Carroll College of Arts and Sciences at Le Moyne College provides a foundation that enables students to navigate a complex and changing world through comprehensive programs in the humanities, arts, and social and natural sciences. Rooted in the Jesuit tradition, the Carroll College prepares students to be discerning, empathetic, curious, and creative citizens who pursue fulfilling and integrated lives.[^chunk:55fe5aca-57eb-5445-9af3-4e0625ce10aa]

### Vision

To seek and to reveal the humanity of the world, and to instill in our students a sense of wonder, a passion for life-long learning, and a deep commitment to the common good. The Carroll College's devotion to *cura personalis* will propel it forward as a preeminent and comprehensive liberal arts hub.[^chunk:55fe5aca-57eb-5445-9af3-4e0625ce10aa]

### Values

Guided by the Ignatian principle of *cura personalis*, the Carroll College values:[^chunk:55fe5aca-57eb-5445-9af3-4e0625ce10aa]

- **Intellectual curiosity**, which drives the critical thinking, growth, and transformation of students, faculty, and staff
- **Engagement with local and global communities**, with a focus on the importance of meaningful relationships
- **Social justice**, promoting equity, diversity, inclusion, and belonging, grounded in commitment to the common good
- **Creativity**, with an emphasis on the contribution of interdisciplinary connections toward empowerment and fulfillment

## How Better Together Advances Le Moyne's Jesuit Liberal Arts Mission

Better Together advances Le Moyne College's Jesuit liberal-arts mission through several key commitments:[^chunk:55fe5aca-57eb-5445-9af3-4e0625ce10aa]

1. **Relationship-Building as Core Practice**: The plan recognizes that developing the whole person depends "not only on the communication of content, but also on the quality of relationships among a wide community of educators and co-learners." Building relationships between and among students, faculty, alumni/ae, staff, and administrators is at the heart of Better Together.

2. **Ignatian Pedagogical Approach**: The plan commits to strengthening opportunities for experiential learning while advancing the apostolic priorities of reconciliation, justice, and care for the common home.

3. **Cultivating Imagination and Practical Application**: The plan honors the Jesuit heritage by emphasizing both the cultivation of imagination and the practical applications of studying the liberal arts.

4. **Collaborative Implementation**: Collaboration with colleagues in the Madden College of Business and Economics, the Purcell School of Professional Studies, and myriad College offices reflects the interconnected nature of a Jesuit education.

## Strategy Constellations

Better Together is organized into three Strategy Constellations:[^chunk:55fe5aca-57eb-5445-9af3-4e0625ce10aa]

### Strategy Constellation 1: Imagination, Growth, and Transformation

This constellation focuses on nurturing the imaginations of faculty, staff, and students, strengthening their sense of community, and supporting their quest to belong.[^chunk:55fe5aca-57eb-5445-9af3-4e0625ce10aa]

**Key initiatives include:**

1. **Establish the Imagination Commons** - A flexible and collaborative space for Carroll College faculty, staff, and students that enables meaningful conversations, iterative thinking, and imaginative teaching, learning, and research (examples: experiential learning, peer-to-peer mentorship, sustainable team teaching, faculty and staff colloquia, micro conferences)

2. **Enhance Carroll College as Liberal Arts Hub** - Strengthen the Carroll College's position as the dynamic liberal arts hub of Le Moyne College and as the driving force of the College's core curriculum by providing new ways for students, faculty, and staff to connect to the liberal arts (examples: early arrival for first-year students and first-year seminar faculty, sustained cohort activities incorporated into core courses, and endowed core professorships)

3. **Support Non-Traditional Students** - Strengthen connections to and support for groups of non-traditional Carroll College students, including adult and senior learners, first-generation students, part-time students, returning students, transfer students, working parents, caregivers, and military-affiliated students

4. **Establish Carroll Communities** - Create new student living and learning communities that support growth and transformation, enable meaningful connections with faculty and alumni/ae mentors, and emphasize student resilience and persistence

5. **Support Scholar-Athletes** - Solidify the Carroll College as a premier destination for scholar-athletes, supporting their academic work through faculty mentorship, dedicated exam proctoring, and scheduling integration

**Foundational Values:** Intellectual curiosity and Social justice

### Strategy Constellation 2: Applying the Liberal Arts

This constellation illuminates the concrete ways a liberal arts education directly benefits students as they move into the world and creates new interdisciplinary credentials, networking opportunities, and research infrastructure.[^chunk:55fe5aca-57eb-5445-9af3-4e0625ce10aa]

**Key initiatives include:**

1. **Center for Applied Liberal Arts and Careers** - Facilitate and support initiatives aimed at articulating the instrumental value of a liberal arts education through new marketing messages and materials that clarify the practical application of a liberal arts education, including essential life skills and specific career outcomes

2. **Applied Liberal Arts Lab** - Include robust tools that support innovative faculty and student research within the liberal arts disciplines (examples: survey tools and statistical software, geospatial mapping systems, a robust media repository, semiconductor and material science equipment, and cybersecurity/artificial intelligence simulation platforms)

3. **New Interdisciplinary Credentials** - Establish new interdisciplinary credentials that integrate the liberal arts and sciences with other disciplines, including collaborations with the Madden College of Business and Economics and the Purcell School of Professional Studies (examples: medical humanities, global business languages, arts and health, public/digital humanities, artificial intelligence and society)

4. **Carroll College Alumni Advisory Council** - Establish a council composed of members from a variety of professional fields who exemplify the successful application of a liberal arts education

5. **Strengthen Career Advising Connections** - Strengthen connections with the office of career advising and development, with an emphasis on continual discernment, facilitation of conversations with alumni/ae, and the support of paid internships for all Carroll College students

6. **Graduate Programs** - Identify and create new revenue-generating graduate programs in fields with emerging workforce needs and strong connections to the liberal arts and sciences

**Foundational Value:** Creativity

### Strategy Constellation 3: Networks of Engagement

This constellation focuses on deeply engaging with surrounding communities, the environment, and global partners toward the vision of being a preeminent liberal arts hub.[^chunk:55fe5aca-57eb-5445-9af3-4e0625ce10aa]

**Key initiatives include:**

1. **Unified Clearinghouse for Travel Experiences** - Create a unified clearinghouse that aggregates resources supporting domestic and international travel-based experiences, including academic experiences as well as experiences organized by Student Development and the Grace Center for Faith and Community

2. **Enhance Community-Engaged Profile** - Enhance the profile of the Carroll College as a preeminent community-engaged liberal arts hub through coordination and marketing of existing programming, as well as new programming and initiatives that engage a variety of stakeholders

3. **Re-establish Service-Learning Understanding** - Facilitate the re-establishment of a shared understanding of service-learning across the Carroll College, in collaboration with the Grace Center for Faith and Community

4. **Strengthen Indigenous Community Relationships** - Strengthen relationships between Carroll College students, faculty, and staff and local indigenous communities through deep and sustained listening

5. **Cross-Generational Learning Communities** - Explore the viability of cross-generational learning communities that actively include older adults, enabling their robust and sustained engagement in classes, lectures, concerts, fieldwork, and other programs

6. **Support CURAR** - Enhance awareness of and support for the Center for Urban and Regional Research (CURAR), with a particular focus on collaborative research conducted by faculty and students

7. **Environmental Programming** - Increase programming and structured conversations focused on environmental change and sustainability, in collaboration with the Center for the Study of Environmental Change

**Foundational Value:** Engagement with local and global communities

## Implementation

To ensure successful implementation, the Carroll College will:[^chunk:55fe5aca-57eb-5445-9af3-4e0625ce10aa]

- Invite faculty, staff, and administrators to join working groups targeting specific aspects of each strategy constellation
- Conduct periodic assessment and reporting on the progress of Better Together to inform stakeholders of successes, status updates, and challenges
- Sustain an institutional culture of continuous assessment and improvement

The envisioned time horizon for this plan is three years, though the rapidly changing world and higher education landscape will require a flexible, rather than a fixed, timeline.[^chunk:3813c17c-ee6f-5cff-a951-7ca3267b0fa3]
