## Founding History and Mission Identity

Saint Joseph's University was founded in 1851 by the Jesuits, opening its doors with approximately 30 young men attending classes next to Old Saint Joseph's Church in Old City, Philadelphia.[^chunk:be86e8d9-ccd0-5dea-b753-064eab7b4f0d] The university has been challenging students to lead engaged lives for more than 170 years, steeping itself in a tradition of academic rigor and social justice.[^chunk:be86e8d9-ccd0-5dea-b753-064eab7b4f0d]

The institution has undergone significant physical and institutional expansion throughout its history. In 1927, Saint Joseph's moved to Hawk Hill in Philadelphia and opened the first building on the City Avenue campus.[^chunk:be86e8d9-ccd0-5dea-b753-064eab7b4f0d] The university welcomed full-time female students in 1970.[^chunk:be86e8d9-ccd0-5dea-b753-064eab7b4f0d] More recently, in 2008, the 38-acre James J. Maguire '58 campus was added to Saint Joseph's in the suburbs on the Main Line.[^chunk:be86e8d9-ccd0-5dea-b753-064eab7b4f0d] In 2010, ground broke on the John R. Post '60 Academic Center and the John and Maryanne Hennings Post Learning Commons, which brought new, collaborative learning environments and a three-story addition to the Drexel Library.[^chunk:be86e8d9-ccd0-5dea-b753-064eab7b4f0d]

The university received significant philanthropic support in 2017 when it announced receipt of a $50 million gift from James J. Maguire '58 and his wife, Frances—the single largest gift in the university's history.[^chunk:be86e8d9-ccd0-5dea-b753-064eab7b4f0d] In 2022, Saint Joseph's merged with University of the Sciences and expanded to a new University City location.[^chunk:be86e8d9-ccd0-5dea-b753-064eab7b4f0d] Most recently, in 2024, Saint Joseph's merged with Pennsylvania College of Health Sciences to provide in-demand program offerings in nursing and allied health.[^chunk:be86e8d9-ccd0-5dea-b753-064eab7b4f0d]

## Initiative for Human Formation in an Age of AI

The Initiative for Human Formation in an Age of AI is a University-wide, multi-year commitment announced by Saint Joseph's University to prepare students, faculty, and staff for a future shaped by artificial intelligence.[^chunk:81794096-93b3-5e4c-81e8-eede3fdc05fc] The initiative was launched in response to the rapid transformation that AI is bringing to education, work, and professional practice.[^chunk:1db83593-eb35-5650-ad0d-fdf970607ec3]

President Cheryl A. McConnell, PhD, emphasized that this is not a marginal technology initiative but rather a fundamental commitment grounded in the University's mission: "Artificial intelligence is changing how students learn, how faculty teach, how employees work and the professions graduates will enter. Saint Joseph's is responding by strengthening what matters most in today's environment: human judgment and the formation of the whole person along with rigorous academic preparation for the workplace of tomorrow."[^chunk:81794096-93b3-5e4c-81e8-eede3fdc05fc]

Artificial intelligence is reshaping the workplace faster than any previous technology, changing entry-level jobs, shifting employer expectations, and transforming which skills are valued.[^chunk:1db83593-eb35-5650-ad0d-fdf970607ec3] The initiative was developed following several months of consultation with the Board of Trustees, alumni, faculty, staff, students, and regional governmental and business leaders, and draws on Pope Leo XIV's encyclical *Magnifica Humanitas: "On Safeguarding the Human Person in the Time of Artificial Intelligence."*[^chunk:81794096-93b3-5e4c-81e8-eede3fdc05fc]

The Initiative takes shape across three interconnected areas: Faculty Preparation (preparing faculty to teach and innovate in an AI-shaped environment), Student Experience (deepening the student experience around core human capacities), and University Operations.[^chunk:81794096-93b3-5e4c-81e8-eede3fdc05fc]

## Ignatian Pedagogy and Mission Week

Saint Joseph's University is guided by the principles of Jesuit education, a holistic approach to learning that integrates intellectual development with moral and spiritual growth.[^chunk:635ff496-128f-5afb-9a87-47e24e515de8] Rooted in the teachings of St. Ignatius of Loyola, Jesuit education seeks to form individuals committed to knowledge, faith, justice, and service.

The core principles that define Ignatian pedagogy at SJU include:

**Faith and Reflection**: Encouraging critical thinking in pursuit of truth.[^chunk:635ff496-128f-5afb-9a87-47e24e515de8]

**Service and Social Justice**: Working toward a just and equitable world.[^chunk:635ff496-128f-5afb-9a87-47e24e515de8]

**Cura Personalis**: Caring for each individual's intellectual, moral, and spiritual development.[^chunk:635ff496-128f-5afb-9a87-47e24e515de8] This principle reflects the personal love and care that educators witness for their students so they might attain the knowledge and freedom to achieve their full potential as young men and women created in God's image and likeness.[^chunk:9a224493-8715-5317-89bc-4400b1d2576a]

**Magis**: Striving for excellence in all that we do.[^chunk:635ff496-128f-5afb-9a87-47e24e515de8] The Magis calls students to become persons of the magis, asking always what more they can do for God's greater honor and glory.[^chunk:9a224493-8715-5317-89bc-4400b1d2576a]

## Mission Priority Examen Process Across Jesuit Universities

The Mission Priority Examen (MPE) is a self-study and peer review process established by the Association of Jesuit Colleges and Universities (AJCU) and the North American Jesuit Provincials to reaffirm the Jesuit, Catholic mission of Jesuit colleges and universities in the United States and Belize.[^chunk:337d04ee-e27c-580d-b39f-38e2a72436f9] The process was requested by the Superior General of the Society of Jesus.[^chunk:337d04ee-e27c-580d-b39f-38e2a72436f9]

The MPE is grounded in the examen, a practice of Ignatian reflection.[^chunk:337d04ee-e27c-580d-b39f-38e2a72436f9] The process invites the campus community to reflect upon areas of strength in embodying Jesuit, Catholic identity and opportunities for deepening institutional mission.[^chunk:337d04ee-e27c-580d-b39f-38e2a72436f9] By welcoming AJCU colleagues to support one another's institutional self-reflection, the process aims to foster a more vibrant and collaborative network of Jesuit higher education.[^chunk:337d04ee-e27c-580d-b39f-38e2a72436f9]

The MPE typically follows a structured timeline spanning multiple months, beginning with a steering committee convening in January and concluding with final submission to AJCU and Society of Jesuits provincial in November.[^chunk:337d04ee-e27c-580d-b39f-38e2a72436f9] The examen process involves broad participation from all sectors of the campus community.[^chunk:337d04ee-e27c-580d-b39f-38e2a72436f9]

## Jesuit Universities as Agents of Social Transformation

Jesuit universities are called upon to be agents of social and spiritual transformation in contemporary society.[^chunk:e4a4773d-2c71-5aa7-938b-dfbb03bba574] This calling reflects a fundamental commitment to entering into the lives of those whose dignity has been stripped away by poverty, racism, displacement, xenophobia, and other dehumanizing forces.[^chunk:e4a4773d-2c71-5aa7-938b-dfbb03bba574]

Fr. Arturo Sosa, S.J., Superior General of the Society of Jesus, articulated this vision in his address to the AJCU Trustee Forum, emphasizing that a university positioned as a venture aimed at transforming society must gravitate toward the margins of society.[^chunk:e4a4773d-2c71-5aa7-938b-dfbb03bba574] Such institutions throw open their doors and windows to people at the margins, who in turn bring with them "a new breath of life that will be a source of life in abundance for all."[^chunk:e4a4773d-2c71-5aa7-938b-dfbb03bba574]

For Jesuit institutions to prioritize work with marginalized communities represents a fundamental reorientation of institutional purpose. It requires moving beyond traditional academic functions to actively engage with those excluded by existing structures and interests that dominate the world.[^chunk:e4a4773d-2c71-5aa7-938b-dfbb03bba574] This commitment extends across institutional operations, as demonstrated by Jesuit universities establishing dedicated offices and divisions focused on mission integration and community engagement.[^chunk:49dc1795-ab73-5478-bfa7-60d746f61e31]
